## Medical Facilities in Quinte West, Ontario: A Comprehensive Directory, Coordinates, and Map Reference  

### Introduction  
Quinte West, located in Hastings County, Ontario, is a region characterized by its proximity to the Trent River and the Bay of Quinte watershed, which influences its environmental, recreational, and infrastructural development. While the area is renowned for its natural waterways, tourism, and historical significance, there is limited public documentation on the distribution and operational status of medical facilities within the municipality. This paper synthesizes available evidence from environmental, infrastructural, and health-related sources to construct a preliminary directory of medical facilities in Quinte West, including their coordinates, map references, and contextual analysis. The findings are contextualized within the broader framework of the Trent River / Bay of Quinte watershed, Hastings County, and proposed development sites such as 7 Riverside Drive (former Saputo Dairy) and 920 Trenton Frankford Road (former Sonoco Paper Mill).

The research methodology involved a systematic review of primary studies, web transcripts, and environmental data from the provided sources. Key focus areas included:
