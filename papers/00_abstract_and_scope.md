# Abstract & Statement of Scope (Version 0.6 — Work in Progress)

**Statement of Neutrality and Public Purpose:** This document is neither for nor against the development of data centers. Rather, its sole objective is to inform the public, municipal decision-makers, and local stakeholders of all currently available technical, environmental, hydrological, acoustic, health, and socioeconomic details surrounding proposed data center developments in the City of Quinte West, Hastings County, and the Trent River / Bay of Quinte watershed. By compiling peer-reviewed scientific literature, provincial utility records, conservation authority filings, and municipal planning documents into a single open-access reference, this assessment seeks to support transparent, evidence-based civic discussion.

**Information Gaps and Living Document Status (v0.6):** Across many critical areas—including finalized electrical feeder allocations, site-specific closed-loop chemical storage inventories, long-term water taking and discharge parameters, low-frequency (dBC) acoustic modeling, and binding municipal tax or community-benefit covenants—publicly disclosed information remains incomplete, preliminary, or unavailable. Consequently, **this document is an active work in progress (Version 0.6)**. Where local site-specific disclosures are absent from the public record, this assessment presents established engineering baselines, empirical case studies, and statutory frameworks from comparable jurisdictions in Ontario, across Canada, and internationally to illustrate the range of potential municipal outcomes.

**Public Submissions and Iterative Revisions:** Because public access to accurate information is an ongoing process, this assessment is maintained in a public Git repository (`https://github.com/cribbageSTARSHIP/quinte_west_data_center_paper`). A dedicated **`public_submissions/`** directory is included in the repository where residents, technical experts, project proponents, and municipal officials can submit additional primary documents, newly released filings, or factual corrections to be merged into subsequent revisions of this paper.
