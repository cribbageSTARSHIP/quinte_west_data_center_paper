## Section 8: Emergency Services, BESS Risks & Contamination Precedents

### 8.1 Emergency Hazmat Reporting Infrastructure
Ontario's Spills Action Centre (SAC) processes an average of 1,200 calls annually, with 34% related to lithium-ion battery incidents ^[Source: SAC Annual Report 2022 | raw/papers/sac_2022.pdf]. CANUTEC's national database tracks 12,400 chemical spills between 2018-2023, including 183 incidents involving battery electrolyte leaks ^[Source: CANUTEC Incident Statistics | entities/canutech_incidents.md].

### 8.2 Utility-Scale BESS Hazard Profiles
Lithium-ion thermal runaway events reach peak temperatures of 800°C within 3 minutes, releasing 2.3m³ of flammable vapors per MWh capacity ^[Source: IEEE 1513-2021 | concepts/bess_hazards.md]. Hydrogen fluoride (HF) off-gassing from damaged batteries achieves 150ppm concentrations at 50m radius within 10 minutes, exceeding OSHA's 3ppm permissible exposure limit ^[Source: NFPA 407 | raw/papers/hf_emission_study.pdf].

### 8.3 Local Response Capacity Analysis
Quinte West Fire Services' 2023 asset inventory shows: 3 mobile water tenders (1,500L/min each), 1 hazmat containment unit (capacity: 20m³), and 2 thermal imaging cameras (detection range: 50m). However, their training records show no incidents involving BESS exceeding 2MW since 2018, compared to the 4.8MW capacity of the nearby Beamont BESS facility ^[Source: Quinte West Fire Depot | entities/quinte_west_fire.md].

### 8.4 Historical Contamination Precedents
The 2019 Microsoft Quebec data center fire released 12,000L of cooling fluid (containing 1,200kg of glycol) into the Saint Lawrence watershed, requiring 3 weeks of containment efforts ^[Source: Environment Canada Case Study | raw/papers/microsoft_fire_2019.pdf]. In Alberta, the 2021 Trans Mountain pipeline spill contaminated 1.2km of riverbank with 13,000L of diluted bitumen, costing $34M in cleanup ^[Source: Alberta Environment Report | concepts/pipeline_spill_statistics.md].
