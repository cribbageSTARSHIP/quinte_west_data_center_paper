# Section 2: Quinte West Watershed & Ecological Baselines

## 2.1 Hydrological Systems
The Quinte West Watershed encompasses four major hydrological systems: the Trent River (flow rate: 12.4 m³/s at Peterborough ^[Source: Trent River Flow Study 2022 | raw/papers/trent_river_flow.pdf]), Bay of Quinte (surface area: 1,270 km² ^[Source: Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources 2020 | entities/bay_of_quinte.md]), Murray Canal (length: 32 km, designed capacity: 18.6 m³/s ^[Source: Lower Trent Conservation Authority 2021 | concepts/murray_canal.md]), and Cold Creek (mean annual discharge: 3.8 m³/s ^[Source: Cold Creek Watershed Report 2019 | raw/papers/cold_creek_watershed.pdf]).

## 2.2 Conservation Authority Jurisdiction
Lower Trent Conservation (LTC) manages three critical wetland complexes:
1. Sager Wetland (area: 1,820 hectares, 34% of which is provincially-designated Source Water Protection Zone ^[Source: LTC Sager Wetland Assessment | entities/sager_wetland.md])
2. Bleasdell Boulder (1,240 hectares, 22% of area classified as provincially significant under the Ontario Wetland Conservation Act ^[Source: Bleasdell Wetland Inventory 2023 | concepts/bleasdell_boulder.md])
3. Murray Marsh (680 hectares, 100% of area protected under the provincial Source Water Protection Program ^[Source: Murray Marsh Ecological Assessment | raw/papers/murray_marsh_ecology.pdf])

## 2.3 Municipal Water Infrastructure
The Quinte West municipality sources drinking water from:
- Trenton Water Treatment Plant (Trent River intake, capacity: 12,000 m³/day ^[Source: City of Quinte West Water Master Plan 2021 | entities/trenton_wtp.md])
- Bayside WTP (Bay of Quinte intake, capacity: 8,500 m³/day ^[Source: Bayside WTP Operational Report 2022 | concepts/bayside_wtp.md])
- Frankford/Batawa Intakes (combined capacity: 3,500 m³/day, located in Class I Source Water Protection Zone ^[Source: Ontario Drinking Water Protection Act 2023 | entities/source_water_zones.md])
Annual treated water production averages 7,000 ML/yr with 98.7% compliance to Canadian Drinking Water Guidelines ^[Source: Quinte West Water Quality Report 2023 | raw/papers/water_quality_2023.pdf].

## 2.4 Ecological Sensitivities
Provincial Source Water Protection Zones cover 42% of the Quinte West watershed area (1,680 km²) with strict land-use restrictions ^[Source: Ontario Ministry of Environment 2022 | entities/source_water_zones.md]. Municipal bylaws include:
- 200m buffer zones around all watercourses (Bylaw 2021-09 ^[Source: City of Quinte West Bylaws | entities/municipal_bylaws.md])
- Noise restrictions (<55 dBA) near wetland areas (Bylaw 2019-12 ^[Source: Noise Control Bylaw 2019 | concepts/noise_regulations.md])
- Pollinator corridor protections in 12km² area across Murray, Sidney, and Frankford wards (Bylaw 2022-05 ^[Source: Apiary Protection Ordinance | entities/apiary_corridors.md])

## 2.5 Wildlife & Agricultural Corridors
The Murray Ward hosts 18 species of provincially threatened wildlife (including 4 endangered amphibians) within 32km² of the Murray Canal ^[Source: Quinte West Biodiversity Assessment 2021 | concepts/murray_wildlife.md]. Sidney Ward contains 14km of designated pollinator corridors (95% of which overlaps with agricultural apiary zones) ^[Source: Pollinator Habitat Map 2023 | raw/papers/pollinator_corridors.pdf]. Frankford Ward's 22km² agricultural area supports 1,200 bee colonies but requires 3.2km of additional hedgerow corridors to meet provincial biodiversity targets ^[Source: Frankford Apiary Study 2022 | entities/frankford_apiary.md].
