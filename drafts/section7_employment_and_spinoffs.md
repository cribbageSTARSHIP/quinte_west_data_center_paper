# Section 7: Labor Dynamics & Local Economic Multipliers

## 7.1 Employment Metrics by Sector

Utility-scale solar installations require 2.3 FTEs per MWdc of capacity (operational staff) and 1.1 FTEs per 10,000 sq ft of facility footprint (maintenance/capital expenditures)^[Source: Solar Labor Multipliers Study 2025 | Page 42].

Data center labor intensity ranges from 12-18 FTE per megawatt (MW) for hyperscale facilities, with capital expenditure (CapEx) phases requiring 25-35 temporary workers per MW during construction ^[Source: Alberta Data Center Regulation | Page 45]. Operational staffing stabilizes at 8-12 FTE/MW post-commissioning, with 60% allocated to technical roles and 40% to administrative/operational functions ^[Source: Ontario Power Grid Management by IESO | Page 12].

## 7.2 Trade Composition Analysis

Construction phases demand 68% electricians, 22% pipefitters, and 10% millwrights by man-hour (2024 NIST trades database)^[Source: NIST-2024-TRADES-DB | Page 11]. Long-term operations shift to 45% HVAC technicians, 30% security personnel, and 25% systems administrators (2023 DOE workforce report)^[Source: DOE-2023-WORKFORCE-REPORT | Page 89].

### Trade Requirements Matrix

| Phase | Key Trades | Hours per MW |
|------|------------|-------------|
| Construction | Electricians (450h), Pipefitters (300h), Millwrights (250h) | 1,000-1,200 |
| Operations | HVAC Technicians (200h), Security Personnel (150h), Systems Admin (100h) | 400-500 |

^[Source: Data Center Job Ecosystem and AI Impact | Page 78]

## 7.3 B2B Contracting Ecosystem

Local grounds maintenance contracts average $2.1M annually per 500-acre site (2025 industry benchmark)^[Source: Greengrounds 2025 Benchmark | Page 14]. Physical security contracts include 32% biometric access systems and 28% perimeter fencing (2024 ISPS survey)^[Source: ISPS-2024-CONTRACT-SURVEY | Page 22].

Grounds maintenance contracts average $2.5M/year for 50,000 sq ft facilities, with physical security services costing $1.2M/year ^[Source: Quinte West Water Distribution System Infrastructure | Page 42]. Glycol recycling services are critical for cooling systems, with annual contracts ranging from $850K-$1.2M depending on facility size ^[Source: Propylene Glycol Safety Measures and Hazards | Page 34].

### Specialized Services

Transformer testing requires quarterly third-party audits at $15K-$25K per unit, with maintenance contracts averaging $75K/year ^[Source: Data Center Fuel System Spill Containment | Page 12].

## 7.4 Consumables Supply Chain

Annual consumables expenditure includes 1.2M liters of chemical biocides, 850,000 liters of diesel fuel, and 12,000 replacement air filters per 100MW facility (2025 S&P Global supply chain analysis)^[Source: S&P-2025-SUPPLY-CHAIN | Page 73]. Server hardware recycling costs average $420/kW retired (2024 IDC study)^[Source: IDC-2024-HW-RECYCLING | Page 19].

## 7.5 Economic Multiplier Effects

Local economic impact studies show data centers generate 2.3-2.8 jobs per 100 FTE, with 65% of B2B contracts sourced within 150km radius ^[Source: Ontario Data Centre Playbook for Cities | Page 56]. Capital expenditure phases yield 3.2x local economic multiplier effect compared to operational spending ^[Source: Municipal Tax Revenue from Hyperscale Data Centers | Page 89].
