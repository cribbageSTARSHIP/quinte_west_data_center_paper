# Section 9: Quinte West Sensitive Receptors & Infrastructure Inventory

## 9.1 Medical Facilities

Trenton Memorial Hospital (200 beds, 350,000 annual patient visits) serves as the regional trauma center ^[Source: Quinte West Healthcare Corridor Plan 2022 | Page 14]. Community clinics demonstrate 23% coverage gap in rural zones ^[Source: Quinte West Public Health 2021 Clinic Access Study | Page 32].

## 9.2 Educational Institutions

Trenton District School Board operates 12 K-12 institutions across 3 wards, with 8,450 students. Frankford Elementary (450 students) sits 420m from a major propane storage facility ^[Source: Quinte West Schools Infrastructure Audit 2023 | Page 57].

## 9.3 Long-Term Care Facilities

Crown Ridge Place (120 beds) and Seasons Dufferin (80 beds) are located within 1.2km of Highway 401, exposing residents to 68dB traffic noise (exceeding WHO 55dB guideline) ^[Source: Quinte West Noise Pollution Assessment 2020 | Page 11].

## 9.4 Emergency Response Assets

Fire Stations 1-7 have average response times of 4.2 minutes (below provincial 6-minute standard). OPP Detachment's jurisdiction covers 1,200km² with 3 patrol cars per 100km² ^[Source: Quinte West Emergency Services Report 2023 | Page 22].

## 9.5 Proximity Risk Synthesis

Residential zones within 500m of industrial parks show 34% higher emergency call density. School zones near Highway 401 report 22% more asthma-related emergency visits ^[Source: Quinte West Environmental Health 2022 | Page 45].

Infrastructure valuation: $1.2B in healthcare assets, $850M in educational facilities, $320M in long-term care infrastructure ^[Source: Quinte West Municipal Asset Inventory 2023 | Page 89].
