# Public Submissions & Community Evidence Portal

This directory is the official public intake portal for **Proposed Hyperscale Data Center Developments in Quinte West: Comprehensive Technical Audit, Watershed Risk Assessment & Regulatory Analysis**.

## Purpose & Neutrality Statement

This research project is **neither for nor against data center development**. Its sole objective is to provide residents, municipal councillors, planners, and stakeholders in the City of Quinte West and Hastings County with a transparent, thoroughly cited synthesis of all available technical, environmental, hydrological, acoustic, and fiscal evidence.

Because significant information gaps currently exist across utility allocations, site-specific engineering specifications, environmental permits, and municipal tax agreements, this document is maintained as a **living work in progress**.

## How to Submit Information for Future Revisions

Members of the public, subject-matter experts, utility engineers, conservation staff, and project proponents are encouraged to submit missing documentation, empirical data, or factual corrections:

1. **Via GitHub Pull Request:**
   - Place primary source documents (PDFs, municipal filings, engineering reports, or freedom-of-information releases) and a completed copy of `SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md` into [`public_submissions/incoming/`](incoming/).
   - Submit a Pull Request to `main`.
2. **Via GitHub Issues:**
   - Open an Issue in this repository attaching the source document or URL and identifying the specific section number (e.g., `Section 1.6.2` or `Section 3.5.1`) the evidence addresses.

## Verification & Merge Standard

Once a submission in `incoming/` is verified against primary documentation, it is moved to `verified_to_merge/`, indexed into the evidentiary archive (`wiki/raw/`), and merged into the next numbered revision of the assessment with full endnote attribution.
