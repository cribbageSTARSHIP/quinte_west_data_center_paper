# Section 7: Employment Metrics and Local Spin-Off Industries

## 7.1: Data Center Staffing Ratios by Facility Size

Data centers require varying numbers of permanent full-time equivalents (FTEs) depending on facility size and type. Edge facilities typically require fewer FTEs per megawatt (MW) compared to hyperscale facilities, which have higher density and complexity. For example, hyperscale facilities may require 10-15 FTEs per MW, while enterprise facilities might require 5-8 FTEs per MW. Additionally, staffing ratios per 100,000 sq ft show similar trends, with hyperscale facilities requiring more personnel due to their larger footprint and advanced infrastructure^[Source: Wikipedia Reference - Data center | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_center].

## 7.2: Internal Job Titles and Technical Roles Inside an Operating Data Center

Operating data centers employ a range of technical roles, including:
- Data Center Manager
- Network Engineer
- Systems Administrator
- IT Security Specialist
- Facilities Engineer
- Power Distribution Technician
- Cooling Systems Engineer
These roles ensure the smooth operation and maintenance of the data center's infrastructure and services^[Source: Wikipedia Reference - Data center | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_center].

## 7.3: Construction and Commissioning Trades Required to Build and Bring a Data Center Online

Building a data center requires specialized construction and commissioning trades, including:
- IBEW Electricians
- UA Pipefitters
- HVAC Sheet Metal Workers
- Millwrights
- Structural Ironworkers
- Commissioning Engineers
These trades are essential for installing and testing the electrical, mechanical, and structural systems required for data center operations^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger their local communities and the country as a whole.pdf, Page 1].

## 7.4: Long-Term Operational and Preventative Maintenance Trades Required

Ongoing maintenance of data centers involves trades such as:
- Electrical Maintenance Technicians
- HVAC Technicians
- Plumbing Technicians
- IT Support Specialists
- Security Systems Technicians
These trades ensure the continued reliability and efficiency of data center operations^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 1].

## 7.5: Local Business and Professional Services Contracted from the Community

Data centers often contract local businesses for services such as:
- 24/7 Physical Security
- Snow Removal
- Groundskeeping
- Hazardous Waste Hauling
- Generator Load-Bank Testing
These contracts support local economies and provide ongoing employment opportunities^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 9].

## 7.6: Consumables and Materials Purchased for Ongoing Operations

Regular consumables and materials required for data center operations include:
- MERV/HEPA Air Filtration
- Inhibited Propylene Glycol
- Ultra-Low-Sulfur Diesel Fuel
- Dielectric Fluids
- VRLA/Li-ion Battery Replacements
- Optical Transceivers
These materials are essential for maintaining the performance and safety of data center systems^[Source: Alberta AI Data Centres Environmental and Regulatory Concerns.pdf, Page 13].
