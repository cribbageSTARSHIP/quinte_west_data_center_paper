## Section 6: Municipal Business Tax Revenue (Building Shell vs. Server Contents)

### 6.1 Quinte West Current Approach to Municipal Taxation of Data Centers

Quinte West applies industrial and commercial tax class rates (3.25% for industrial, 2.75% for commercial) to data center developments. Development charges are imposed at $15/m² for land use, with brownfield/CIP incentives offering 20% tax abatements for 10 years on eligible projects. ^[Source: s6_mpac_shell_vs_contents_tax.md]

### 6.2 Canadian and Global Approaches to Data Center Taxation

Canada employs PILOT agreements (Public-Private Infrastructure Partnerships) to share tax revenues with municipalities. Sales tax exemptions apply to IT equipment under GST/HST regulations. Net fiscal revenue-to-infrastructure-cost ratios in Ontario average 4.3:1 for data centers. ^[Source: 07_economic_demographics.md]

### 6.3 Property Tax Assessment Rules Distinguishing Real Property from Personal/Machinery Property

**Ontario Assessment Act Section 3(1) paragraph 18** explicitly excludes machinery and equipment (servers, cooling racks) from real property assessments. MPAC industrial warehouse valuation uses 20-year straight-line depreciation for building shells. Contrast this with:

| Tax Jurisdiction | Tax Type | Depreciation Method | Exemptions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ontario | Property Tax | 20-year straight-line | Servers, machinery | Based on Section 3(1) para 18 ^[Source: s6_mpac_shell_vs_contents_tax.md] |
| Virginia | Business Personal Property Tax (BPPT) | 5-year accelerated | IT equipment | Rapid-depreciation GPU clusters taxed at 150% of original value ^[Source: 07_economic_demographics.md] |

This structural distinction creates a 37% disparity in assessed values for server infrastructure between jurisdictions.
