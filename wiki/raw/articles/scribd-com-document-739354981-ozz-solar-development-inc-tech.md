OZZ Solar Proposal for Toronto PV Projects
OZZ Solar Development Inc. is proposing turn-key engineering, procurement, and construction of 5 solar PV carport projects totaling 1.276 MW DC and 11 solar PV rooftop projects totaling 801.6 kW DC for the City of Toronto. OZZ Solar has over 11 years of experience developing and constructing over 200 commercial solar projects in Ontario totaling approximately 50MW.
