# Source Content

A new Best Practice Guide for Canadian Data Centres is now available through the National Energy Use Database (NEUD) publications page.
This guide was created to help data centre operators across Canada improve energy efficiency, lower operating costs, and reduce their environmental impact. It includes practical tips based on international best practices, all tailored for the Canadian context.
As demand for digital services continues to grow, data centres are using more and more electricity. This guide supports operators in making sustainable changes, like installing smart metering and tracking energy performance so they can keep up with future trends in an evolving sector.
Explore the Best Practice Guide for Canadian Data Centres and start saving energy!
