# Source Content

Utility providers for electricity, natural gas, municipal water and waste water and telecom vary throughout the Bay of Quinte region. As part of your site selection process, our experienced team works with you on providing the most up-to-date information specific to your project.
Contact our team for more site specific information:
- Electricity providers in the Bay of Quinte region are Hydro One and Elexicon.
- Natural Gas in the Bay of Quinte is provided by Enbridge Gas
- Municipal Water & Sewer is offered by each City or Municipality
- Telecom providers – Cogeco and Bell Canada, Rogers and others
Reach out to the QEDC team for more site specific information related to your project.
