Trenton Council Introduces Legislation Ahead of November Data Center Vote
Trenton City Council introduced legislation outlining proposed tax breaks, limits on future levies and a YMCA discount tied to the outcome of November's Issue 17 data center vote. An Ohio policy expert says the unusual structure could potentially lead to litigation after the election.
