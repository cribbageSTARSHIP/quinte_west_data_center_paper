# Spills Action Centre

**Contact**: 1-800-466-2266 (24/7)
**Functions**:
- Centralized spill reporting hub
- Coordinates provincial, municipal, and private responses
- Tracks cleanup activities
- Provides environmental health advice

**Regulatory Role**:
- Enforces Ontario Regulation 675/98
- May recover cleanup costs from responsible parties
- Works with:
  - Local municipalities
  - Pollutant owners
  - Emergency responders
