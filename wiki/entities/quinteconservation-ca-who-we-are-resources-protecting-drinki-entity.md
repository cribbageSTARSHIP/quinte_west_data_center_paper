Quinte Conservation Authority (QCA) is a regional conservation authority responsible for managing water resources and environmental protection in Quinte West, Ontario. Key responsibilities include:

- Monitoring groundwater quality across 1,200 km² watershed
- Enforcing well drilling regulations (requires licensed professionals)
- Providing drought mitigation guidance to 3,500+ private well users
- Operating 8 water quality testing labs across the region
- Maintaining a database of 12,000+ private well records
