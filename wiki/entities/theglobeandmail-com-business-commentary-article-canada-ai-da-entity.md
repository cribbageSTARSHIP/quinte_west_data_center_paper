This page documents the *Data Centres: Sharing the Load* report, a collaborative effort by industry experts including Kathleen Kauth (Mantle Climate) and Tyler Hamilton (MaRS Discovery District). The report examines the economic and energy implications of AI data center growth across Canada and proposes frameworks for responsible development.

### Key Contributions
- **Policy Framework**: Advocates for data center projects that address community concerns through:
  - Grid infrastructure upgrades that benefit residents
  - Sustainable water management practices
  - Local job creation and Canadian innovation support
  - Transparent performance metrics reporting

### Regional Impact
The report has influenced Ontario's new *Data Centre Playbook*, which seeks to balance AI infrastructure development with community needs. Similar strategies are being adopted in U.S. states like Michigan, Texas, and Virginia, which tie tax incentives to clear expectations for community benefits.
