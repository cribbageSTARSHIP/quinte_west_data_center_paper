TELUS (TSX:T) is a major Canadian telecom provider offering wireless, internet, fibre, health, security, and digital services. Key facts:

- Operates one of Canada's largest telecom networks
- Has 19% free cash flow growth to $583M in Q1 2026
- Maintains $0.42 quarterly dividend (10.2% yield)
- Develops AI infrastructure through Sovereign AI Factories (Rimouski and Kamloops facilities)
- Faces challenges with debt management and telecom competition
