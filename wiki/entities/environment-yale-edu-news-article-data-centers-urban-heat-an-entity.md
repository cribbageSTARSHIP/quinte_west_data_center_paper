Yale School of the Environment (YSE) is a research institution focused on environmental science, policy, and sustainability. It has conducted studies on data center impacts, including waste heat emissions, urban heat island effects, and environmental trade-offs of AI infrastructure. Key researchers include Karen Seto (urbanization science), Daniel Esty (environmental law), Kenneth Gillingham (energy economics), and Yuan Yao (industrial ecology).
