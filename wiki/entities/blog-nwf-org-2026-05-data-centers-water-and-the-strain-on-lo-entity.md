# National Wildlife Federation (NWF)

NWF is a conservation organization advocating for environmental protections related to data center expansion. They highlight:

- Water use transparency issues [[1]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/)
- Ecological impacts of water extraction [[2]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/)
- Need for watershed-level water regulation [[3]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/)
