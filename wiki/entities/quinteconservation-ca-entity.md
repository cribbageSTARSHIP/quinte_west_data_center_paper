**Quinte Conservation** is a regional conservation authority serving the Bay of Quinte watershed. Key responsibilities include:

- Managing 15 dams across Moira and Napanee rivers
- Conducting invasive species control (Water Soldier eradication program)
- Administering the Jamie Prud'Homme Memorial Fund for outdoor education
- Leading environmental assessments for infrastructure projects

Recent activities:
- Herbicide application for Water Soldier (Oct 2026)
- Annual dam operations (post-Oct 13, 2026)
- Transition planning to Eastern Lake Ontario Regional Conservation Authority (ELORCA) effective Feb 2027
