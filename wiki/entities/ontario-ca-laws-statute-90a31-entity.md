# Ontario e-Laws Platform

The official legal document repository for Ontario requires JavaScript enforcement for proper functionality. This platform provides:

- Access to Statute 90A31 and related emergency protocols
- Secure document rendering for [[concepts/08-hazards-emergency.md]]
- Integration with [[entities/ieso-ca-en-learn-ontario-power-system-overview-of-the-grid-entity.md]] regulatory systems
