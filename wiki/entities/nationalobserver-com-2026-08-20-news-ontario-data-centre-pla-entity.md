# Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO)

## Overview
The AMO represents 444 municipalities across Ontario, providing advocacy and resources for local governments. In the context of data center development, AMO:

- Publishes model bylaws for data center land use
- Maintains a [Data Centre Development Toolkit](https://www.amo.ca/data-centre-development-toolkit)
- Provides technical assistance on tax incentive frameworks

## Key Positions
1. Supports municipal discretion in negotiating tax abatements
2. Advocates for 100% developer-funded infrastructure upgrades
3. Requires environmental assessments for all new facilities > 10 MW
