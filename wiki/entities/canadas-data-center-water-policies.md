### Canada's Data Center Water Policies

Canada's Responsible Data Centre Development Principles emphasize:
1. Minimizing freshwater consumption and protecting local water supplies
2. Prioritizing sustainable water technologies like closed-loop systems
3. Accounting for local water resource constraints

Key requirements:
- Data centers must not compromise local water availability
- Must use high-efficiency water technologies
- Must consider environmental performance in water management
