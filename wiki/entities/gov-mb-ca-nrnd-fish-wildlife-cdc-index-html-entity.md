The Manitoba Conservation Data Centre (MBCDC), established in 1994, is a partnership between The Manitoba Museum, The Nature Conservancy (TNC), and Natural Resources and Indigenous Futures. It functions under NatureServe Canada, maintaining biodiversity data on Manitoba's species and communities. The MBCDC provides species lists (plant, animal, insect, and S1-S3 ranked species), maps element occurrences, and supports developers with conservation guidelines. ^[Source: Conservation Data Centre | https://www.gov.mb.ca/nrnd/fish-wildlife/cdc/index.html] [[concepts/gov-mb-ca-nrnd-fish-wildlife-cdc-index-html-concept.md]] [[entities/national-wildlife-federation-entity.md]]
