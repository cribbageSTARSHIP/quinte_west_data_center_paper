Canada's Responsible Data Centre Development Principles require:

1. Environmental impact assessments covering power use, water consumption, sound, and emissions (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 3])
2. Long-term strategic value through tax revenue, Canadian supply-chain participation, and workforce development (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 3])
3. Compliance with cybersecurity and continuity of service requirements for sensitive data (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 3])
