# Quinte West Data Center Proposals

## Key Projects
- **Trenton Data Center (Riverside Drive)**
  - Proposed small-scale facility under rezoning review
  - Community concerns: environmental impact, traffic, and infrastructure strain
  ^[Source: Quinte News | https://www.quintenews.com/2026/09/11/rezoning-recommendation-for-trenton-data-centre/]
- **Former Saputo Plant Site (Trenton)**
  - Deferred decision by Quinte West Council in 2026
  - Potential for colocation or cloud infrastructure
  ^[Source: InQuinte | https://www.inquinte.ca/2026/09/proposed-data-centre-sparks-tension-and-confusion-at-quinte-west-council/]

## Regional Context
- Ontario's Data Centre Tracker lists Quinte West as a potential site for new infrastructure
  ^[Source: Ontario Data Centre Tracker | https://ontariodatacenters.ca/]
