**Overview**: Essex County Council is evaluating a municipal framework to regulate AI data centre development, focusing on land zoning policies and resource impact assessments. Initiated by Kingsville's deputy mayor Kimberly DeYong, this initiative seeks to proactively address potential applications while balancing economic opportunities with environmental and community concerns.

**Key Details**: 
- **Motion Status**: Pending debate on Wednesday evening regarding vulnerable land zoning policies.
- **Public Engagement**: Received ~150 resident emails expressing concerns about resource usage (water/energy) and potential economic impacts.
- **Strategic Considerations**: 
  - Need for comprehensive planning tools to assess data centre requirements.
  - Potential zoning restrictions may conflict with other industrial operations.
  - Emphasis on public consultations to inform policy decisions.
