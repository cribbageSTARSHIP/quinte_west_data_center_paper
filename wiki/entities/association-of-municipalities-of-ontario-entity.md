# Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO)

AMO represents over 2,000 municipalities across Ontario and advocates for local government interests. Key positions from their 2026 policy brief on property tax and the National Housing Strategy:

- **Tax Revenue Concerns**: Warns federal housing subsidies may reduce municipal tax bases while increasing infrastructure maintenance costs. ^[Source: Title | https://www.amo.on.ca/policy/finance-infrastructure-and-economy/property-tax-reassessment-national-housing-strategy]

- **Policy Advocacy**: Calls for federal-provincial-municipal coordination to ensure housing programs don't undermine local fiscal sustainability. ^[Source: Title | https://www.amo.on.ca/policy/finance-infrastructure-and-economy/property-tax-reassessment-national-housing-strategy]

- **Infrastructure Funding**: Highlights risks of cost-shifting for affordable housing projects without adequate federal infrastructure contributions. ^[Source: Title | https://www.amo.on.ca/policy/finance-infrastructure-and-economy/property-tax-reassessment-national-housing-strategy]
