# Union of BC Municipalities (UBCM)

The Union of BC Municipalities (UBCM) is advocating for tax reform to reclassify AI data centers as light industry rather than regular businesses. This would significantly increase municipal tax revenue from these facilities, helping cover infrastructure costs and environmental impacts.

Key activities:
- Endorsed Mackenzie's call for tax reform
- Works with BC Assessment to address property classification
- Represents 200+ municipalities in BC
- Focuses on fiscal sustainability and data center regulation
