**OZZ Solar Development Inc.** is a Canadian solar project developer with over **11 years of experience** and **200+ commercial solar projects** completed in Ontario (50 MW total capacity). The company is currently proposing **1.276 MW DC** of solar PV carport installations and **801.6 kW DC** of rooftop projects for the City of Toronto.
