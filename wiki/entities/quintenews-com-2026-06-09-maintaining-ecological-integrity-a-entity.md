**Sandbanks Provincial Park** is a protected area in Ontario, Canada, renowned for its:
- World's largest freshwater bay mouth barrier dune system
- Conservation efforts focused on the dune ecosystem
- Volunteer programs for invasive species removal
- Educational initiatives to promote ecological integrity

Key management practices include:
- Post-and-rope barriers to protect sensitive dune areas (^[Source: Title | https://www.quintenews.com/2026/06/09/maintaining-ecological-integrity-at-ontario-parks/])
- Annual Buckthorn Removal Weekends by Friends of the Sandbanks
- Native plant restoration (maram grass, milkweed) (^[Source: Title | https://www.quintenews.com/2026/06/09/maintaining-ecological-integrity-at-ontario-parks/])
