# Alberta Data Centre Regulatory Authority

The Alberta government maintains a comprehensive regulatory framework for large-scale data centre development, characterized by:

- **Zero public subsidies**: No tax breaks, power discounts, or direct financial incentives are provided to data centre developers [[1]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

- **Data Centre Levy**: A 2% levy on computing equipment value, fully deductible against Alberta corporate income taxes, applies to large-scale operations [[2]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

- **Land and Resource Controls**: Foreign ownership of land is restricted by the Agricultural and Recreational Land Ownership Act, with data centres directed to industrial land [[3]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

- **Multi-tier Approval Process**: Projects require approvals from: local councils (zoning), Alberta Utilities Commission (power), environmental regulators, and grid operators - any single entity can block a project [[4]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

- **Value Capture Mechanism**: Alberta retains economic benefits through royalties, property taxes, and infrastructure development while requiring private sector investment in power plants, pipelines, and fibre routes [[5]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).
