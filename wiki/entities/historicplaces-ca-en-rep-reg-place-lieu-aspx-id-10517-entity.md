The Sidney Electric Power Company historically leased the dam at Sidney Lock Station, which was originally constructed to enable passage around a dam on the Trent-Severn Waterway. This dam was integral to the waterway’s function, allowing boats to navigate around obstacles. The company’s involvement with the dam highlights the intersection of industrial infrastructure and historic preservation, as the Sidney Transformer Station (now owned by Ontario Hydro) continues to provide power to Trenton. 

The company’s legacy is tied to the Lockmaster’s House, which served as both a residence for the lockmaster and an operational hub for managing the lock and dam systems. This connection underscores the role of utilities in shaping and maintaining historic infrastructure.
