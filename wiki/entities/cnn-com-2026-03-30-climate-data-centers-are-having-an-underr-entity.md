The University of Cambridge's Earth Observation group, led by Andrea Marinoni, conducted a study on heat islands from AI data centers. Their research found significant temperature increases near hyperscale facilities, impacting 340 million people [[wikilinks]](concepts/cnn-com-2026-03-30-climate-data-centers-are-having-an-underr-concept.md).
