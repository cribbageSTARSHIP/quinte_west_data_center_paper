### Organization Overview
Gascon is a Quebec-based law firm specializing in real estate and municipal taxation law, with particular expertise in data centre legal issues. The firm represents:

- Data centre operators negotiating lease terms
- Municipalities assessing property tax frameworks
- Industry groups advocating for tax policy changes

### Notable Legal Contributions
- Authored the 2026 analysis on immovable property classification for data centres
- Represented Quebec Employers Council in lobbying efforts
- Advised on lease negotiations for high-value data centre infrastructure
