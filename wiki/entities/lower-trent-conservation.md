# Lower Trent Conservation

## Watershed Jurisdiction
Lower Trent Conservation (LTC) oversees the **Trent River watershed** from the **Source Protection Plan** (2015) to prevent contamination of drinking water intakes. It spans five Conservation Authority areas: Lower Trent, Crowe Valley, Ganaraska Region, Kawartha Region, and Otonabee.

^[Source: raw/articles/trent_river_intakes_and_ipz_source.md, Page 11]

## Conservation Reserves
Key protected areas include:
- **Bleasdell Boulder**: A glacial landform critical for groundwater recharge.
- **Sager Conservation Area**: Protects riparian zones along the Trent River.

^[Source: raw/articles/trent_river_intakes_and_ipz_source.md, Page 14]

## Source Protection Authority Mandate
LTC enforces the **Clean Water Act** to:
1. **Map vulnerable areas** (IPZ-1, IPZ-2, IPZ-3)
2. **Regulate industrial activities** near intakes (e.g., hazardous waste storage permits)
3. **Monitor groundwater quality** in Wellhead Protection Areas (WHPA)
