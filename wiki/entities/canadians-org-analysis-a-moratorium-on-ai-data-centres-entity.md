# The Council of Canadians

## Overview
The Council of Canadians is a national environmental and social justice organization that advocates for sustainable policies. They led a campaign requesting a moratorium on hyperscale AI data centre development in Alberta, Canada, due to environmental, health, and socioeconomic concerns.

## Key Activities

### Environmental Advocacy
^[Source: https://canadians.org/analysis/a-moratorium-on-ai-data-centres/]
They highlighted the significant power demands, natural gas dependency, water consumption, and lack of transparency in data centre projects. Their campaign includes 52 organizations, including Alberta Wilderness Association and Calgary Climate Hub.

### Policy Recommendations
^[Source: https://canadians.org/analysis/a-moratorium-on-ai-data-centres/]
They requested:
1. A moratorium on data centre development until full impacts are known.
2. Mandatory Environmental Impact Assessments (EIAs) for all projects.
3. Transparent tax policies and economic benefit analysis.

## Impact
Their efforts aim to protect Alberta's watersheds, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, and ensure public engagement in decision-making processes. They reference existing moratoriums on coal and renewable energy development in Alberta as precedents.
