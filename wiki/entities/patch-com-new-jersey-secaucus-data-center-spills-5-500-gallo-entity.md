Equinix operates a 25-year-old data center on Hartz Way in Secaucus, NJ, currently expanding its facilities. The site experienced a 5,500-gallon diesel spill from on-site storage tanks used for backup generators. The NJ DEP confirmed no wildlife impact, but cleanup continued through Sunday. The company faces potential fines for the spill, which occurred during construction activities.
