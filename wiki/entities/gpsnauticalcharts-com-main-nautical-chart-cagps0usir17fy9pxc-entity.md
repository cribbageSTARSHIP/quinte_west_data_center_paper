GPSNauticalCharts is a provider of digital nautical charts and maritime navigation software. The organization specializes in creating high-resolution marine charts for freshwater and coastal environments, with a focus on Great Lakes regions. Their products include:

1. **i-Hunting Topo Maps** - Multi-platform navigation apps for iPhone, iPad, MacBook, and Windows
2. **Marine ChartPlotter** - Advanced navigation software with wind speed/direction measurement capabilities ^[Source: Title | https://www.gpsnauticalcharts.com/main/nautical-chart/cagps0usir17fy9pxc1yqjpp-lake-ontario-cfb-trenton-quinte-west-harbour-chart-1-9-000-nautical-chart.html]
3. **Nautical Chart App** - Features include fishing spots, depth contours, and real-time wind meteograms

The organization's services are relevant to maritime safety and navigation infrastructure in Quinte West and other regions. [[concepts/gpsnauticalcharts-com-main-nautical-chart-cagps0usir17fy9pxc-concept.md]]
