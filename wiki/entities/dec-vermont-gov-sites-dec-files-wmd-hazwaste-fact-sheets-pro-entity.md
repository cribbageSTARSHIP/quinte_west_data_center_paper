# Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation

## Core Responsibilities
- Regulating hazardous waste under RCRA and state statutes
- Enforcing groundwater protection standards (Vt. Stat. Title 10 § 4601)
- Overseeing industrial hazardous waste permits

## Key Programs
- Universal Waste Program (2003): Streamlined handling of batteries, pesticides, etc.
- Corrective Action Program: Site remediation for hazardous waste releases
- Emergency Response Division: 24/7 hazardous material spill response
