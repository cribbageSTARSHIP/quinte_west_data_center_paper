# Loudoun County Government Entity

## Noise Regulations
- Zoning Ordinance requires pre-construction and post-construction noise studies
- Maximum allowable sound levels: 55 dBA at property lines for residential areas
- Noise complaints handled by Zoning Enforcement Division
- Amendments to Comprehensive Plan and Zoning Ordinance anticipated 2027

## Air Quality Management
- DEQ mandates Tier 4-equivalent emissions standards for new generators
- Ongoing air quality monitoring in "data center alley" near Ashburn
- No authority to regulate exterior air quality (managed by Virginia DEQ)
