The Fred Meyer Distribution Center in Clackamas, Oregon is a retail logistics facility that experienced a 2014 propylene glycol spill incident. Key details include:

- **Incident**: Contractors accidentally poured 120 gallons of propylene glycol into a storm drain on August 21, 2014, leading to contamination of Carli Creek.
- **Cleanup**: Fred Meyer hired an environmental firm to remove the chemical using vacuum trucks and absorbent materials, with operations concluding on August 27, 2014.
- **Environmental Impact**: Initial tests showed propylene glycol was no longer detectable in Carli Creek by August 22, 2014, though 1,300 gallons of liquid and five drums were processed for off-site disposal.
