Alberta's data center regulatory framework faces criticism for insufficient environmental oversight. Key issues include:

1. **Lack of EA requirements** for data centers under EPEA (Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act), despite impacts on land, water, and air (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
2. **Water licensing exemptions** allowing data centers to piggyback on municipal licenses (Wonder Valley case) (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
3. **No mandatory reclamation** requirements for decommissioned sites (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
4. **No transparency** in water usage with current licensing model (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
