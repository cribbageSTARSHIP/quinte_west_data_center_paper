# EH Science (ehsciences.org)

Nonprofit research organization focused on AI infrastructure health impacts. Key activities include:

- Conducting peer-reviewed studies on data center-induced cardiovascular stress patterns
- Mapping PFAS contamination risks near hyperscale facilities
- Developing acoustic measurement protocols for low-frequency emissions

Maintains the [AI Data Center Health Impacts](https://ehsciences.org/ai-data-center-health-impacts/) research ledger, which documents:

- Sleep disturbance thresholds from 24/7 data center operations
- Correlations between particulate plumes and asthma prevalence
- Thermal discharge effects on local healthcare systems
