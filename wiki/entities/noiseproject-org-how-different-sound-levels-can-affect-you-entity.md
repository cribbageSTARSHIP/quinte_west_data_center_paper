# Noise Project

## Overview
The Noise Project is an organization dedicated to researching and mitigating the impacts of noise pollution on human health and the environment. They provide tools like the Noise Project app to measure sound levels and map noise refuges.

## Key Activities
- Research on noise pollution effects
- Development of the Noise Project app for sound level measurement
- Advocacy for noise pollution policy
- Public education on noise health impacts
