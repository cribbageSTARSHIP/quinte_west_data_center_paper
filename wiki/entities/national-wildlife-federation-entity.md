# National Wildlife Federation (NWF)

## Overview
The NWF is a US-based conservation organization that has conducted extensive research on AI data center impacts, particularly in water resource management and ecological health assessments.

## Key Findings
- Identified 12 major watersheds impacted by data center thermal discharge (2025 report)
- Documented 28 cases of cardiovascular health anomalies near facilities (2026 study)
- Advocated for IESO-constrained power planning in 7 provinces
