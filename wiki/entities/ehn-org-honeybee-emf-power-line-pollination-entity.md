**Overview:**
Environmental Health News (EHN) is a nonprofit organization focused on investigating environmental health impacts of industrial infrastructure, including AI data centers and power transmission systems.

**Key Activities:**
- Publishes peer-reviewed research on EMF effects on wildlife (e.g., [2023 study on honeybees](https://www.ehn.org/honeybee-emf-power-line-pollination)).
- Advocates for updated federal safety standards for non-ionizing radiation.
- Collaborates with researchers to document ecological impacts of high-voltage power lines.
