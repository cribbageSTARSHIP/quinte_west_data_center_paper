Acoustical Consultants Inc. is a leading acoustics engineering firm specializing in noise investigations, environmental assessments, and sound measurement standards. Their work includes:

- Developing A-weighting (dBA) and C-weighting (dBC) measurement protocols for industrial and commercial noise assessments
- Providing noise control solutions for data centers, manufacturing facilities, and urban environments
- Conducting sound level measurements and acoustic simulations
