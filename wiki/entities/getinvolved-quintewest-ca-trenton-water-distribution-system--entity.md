# Trenton Water Distribution System

## Core Infrastructure
- **Treatment Plant**: Conventional chemically assisted filtration plant with 35,800 cu.m/day capacity
- **Storage Tanks**: Two pressure zones with 2,273 cu.m capacity each (Oak Street and 2nd Dug Hill Road)
- **Booster Stations**: Five total including Mount Pelion (replacement planned), Catherine Street, Telephone Road (serves 401 ONroute), Young’s Cove Fire, and Trenton-Frankford

## Service Area
- **Population**: 27,800 residents in Trenton, plus CFB Trenton and parts of Prince Edward County
- **Connections**: Underwater pipes link to Bayside and Carrying Place/Consecon systems

## Upgrades
- **Mount Pelion Station**: Replacement planned to increase capacity and reliability
- **Master Plan Alignment**: Upgrades align with the City’s Official Plan and Water/Wastewater Master Plan [[concepts/getinvolved-quintewest-ca-trenton-water-distribution-system--concept.md]]
