# Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO)

^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-得-know]

AMO represents Ontario's municipalities in provincial policy discussions, including data centre governance. Key roles include:

- Advocating for municipal input in the Data Centre Playbook.
- Supporting studies on AI-scale data centre impacts (^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-need-know]).

AMO's involvement highlights tensions between provincial grid approval authority and municipal land-use planning responsibilities (^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-need-know]).
