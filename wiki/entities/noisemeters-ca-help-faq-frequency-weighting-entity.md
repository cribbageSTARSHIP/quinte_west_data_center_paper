Noisemeters.ca is a Canadian organization specializing in sound measurement technology and standards. They provide authoritative guidance on frequency weighting for sound level meters, explaining:

1. The relationship between human auditory perception and A/C/Z-weighting
2. The importance of correct weighting in noise measurement (e.g., 40 dB error possible with incorrect weighting for 31 Hz tones)
3. Compliance with international standards (IEC 60651, IEC 60804, IEC 61672, ANSI S1.4)

Their resources are frequently cited in environmental noise assessment and industrial acoustics applications. [[concepts/noisemeters-ca-help-faq-frequency-weighting-concept.md]]
