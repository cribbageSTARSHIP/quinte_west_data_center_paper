# Policy Alternatives Canada

## Organization Overview

Policy Alternatives Canada is a non-profit research institute focused on analyzing the socioeconomic and environmental impacts of large-scale infrastructure projects, including AI data centers.

## Key Activities

- Conducts policy research on municipal tax revenue implications of data center development
- Analyzes emergency preparedness requirements for hyperscale facilities
- Publishes regional impact assessments (see [[concepts/policyalternatives-ca-news-research-so-youre-getting-a-data--concept.md]])
