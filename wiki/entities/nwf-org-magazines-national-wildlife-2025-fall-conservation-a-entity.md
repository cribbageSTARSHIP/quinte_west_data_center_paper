The National Wildlife Federation (NWF) is a conservation organization publishing the National Wildlife magazine. Their 2025 Fall issue features an article on AI data center environmental impacts, highlighting energy/water usage, pollution risks, and sustainable solutions. The NWF collaborates with researchers and policymakers to address ecological challenges from technological expansion.^[Source: Title | https://www.nwf.org/Magazines/National-Wildlife/2025/Fall/Conservation/AI-Data-Centers]
