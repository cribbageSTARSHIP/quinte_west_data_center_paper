The **B.C. Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Strategy (ENV)** is responsible for overseeing environmental protection and spill response in British Columbia. In the **Rivers Inlet Diesel Spill** incident, the ministry:

- Coordinated with **Wuikinuxv First Nation** and **Heiltsuk First Nation** to identify culturally and environmentally sensitive sites^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- Deployed **Environmental Emergency Response Officers (EEROs)** to monitor the spill site and collaborate with the **Canadian Coast Guard (CCG)**^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- Oversaw cleanup efforts, including the removal of contaminated diesel and the development of soil remediation plans^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].

### Key Responsibilities
- Enforces environmental regulations and spill response protocols.
- Works with federal agencies like **Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC)** and **Transport Canada** for spill trajectory modeling and resource protection.
- Engages with Indigenous communities to ensure culturally sensitive spill management.
