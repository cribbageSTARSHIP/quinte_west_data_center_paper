# Acoustical Consultants Inc.

## Overview
Acoustical Consultants Inc. is a leading provider of noise assessment and acoustic engineering services, specializing in:
- Environmental impact studies
- Noise pollution mitigation
- Sound measurement instrumentation
- Regulatory compliance consulting

## Key Services
- **Field Measurements:** dBA/dBC sound level monitoring using Class 1 instrumentation^[Source: https://sounddbmeter.com/dba-vs-dbc/].
- **Data Analysis:** Frequency-weighting comparisons for industrial and commercial clients.
- **Regulatory Reporting:** ISO 1996-compliant noise assessments for municipal planning.

## Industry Recognition
- Featured in Noise Control Engineering Journal (NCEJ) for innovative low-frequency measurement techniques.
- Partner organization for the Acoustical Society of America (ASA) training programs.
