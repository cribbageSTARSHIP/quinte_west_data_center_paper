# Centre for AI, Trust and Governance, The University of Sydney

Led by Dr. Rob Nicholls, this center produced the report *The Digital Climate Footprint: From Global Data Centre Growth to Local Ecological Consequences*. Key activities include:

- Researching the environmental impacts of AI on ecosystems, including bees (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
- Advocating for **carbon-aware computing** and AI model efficiency standards (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
- Collaborating with IBM Australia on sustainable AI innovation (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
