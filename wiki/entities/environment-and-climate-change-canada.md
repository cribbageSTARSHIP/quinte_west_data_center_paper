The Department of Environment and Climate Change Canada oversees national air quality monitoring programs, including:

- Air pollutant emissions tracking since 1990
- Regulatory frameworks for industrial emissions control
- Environmental indicator reporting on SOX, NOX, PM2.5, and other pollutants

See [[concepts/air-pollutant-emissions-in-canada]] for detailed data analysis and [[concepts/responsible-data-centre-development]] for mitigation strategies.
