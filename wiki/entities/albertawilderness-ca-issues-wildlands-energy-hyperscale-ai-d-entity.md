# Alberta Wilderness Association (AWA)

AWA is a leading environmental organization in Alberta advocating for moratoriums on AI hyperscale data centre development. Key activities include:

- Coordinating a joint letter with 20+ environmental and community groups to halt unassessed projects.
- Highlighting risks of bypassing Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA) for projects like Mihta Askiy, Synapse, and Wonder Valley.
- Exposing internal federal briefings promoting data centres as natural gas market boosters.
