# Municipal Property Assessment Corporation (MPAC)

The Municipal Property Assessment Corporation (MPAC) is responsible for accurately assessing and classifying property in Ontario for the purposes of municipal and education taxes. MPAC updates property assessments every four years, with the latest update reflecting the valuation date of January 1, 2016.

## Role and Responsibilities

- **Assessment**: MPAC assesses and classifies property in Ontario according to the Assessment Act and its associated regulations.
- **Legislation**: Governed by the Assessment Act, which requires that land be assessed based on its current value, defined as the amount of money the fee simple, if unencumbered, would realize if sold at arm's length by a willing seller to a willing buyer.
- **Classification**: Properties are classified into the Industrial Property Class or Commercial Property Class based on their use, as outlined in Ontario Regulation 282/98.

## Key Activities

- **Data Collection and Analysis**: MPAC collects and analyzes data on property sales, land values, and market trends to ensure accurate assessments.
- **Valuation Methodology**: The valuation process involves the cost approach, considering reproduction cost new, physical deterioration, functional obsolescence, and external obsolescence.
