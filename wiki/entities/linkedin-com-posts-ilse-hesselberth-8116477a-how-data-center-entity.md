# Equinix Secaucus Data Center Spill Incident

## Facility Profile
Located in Secaucus, New Jersey, this Equinix facility houses critical infrastructure for cloud computing operations. The 2026 diesel fuel spill (3,500 gallons) from backup generators exposed gaps in data center environmental safeguards.

## Incident Details
- **Date**: 2026-09-15
- **Volume**: 3,500 gallons diesel
- **Location**: Hackensack River tributary
- **Cause**: Failed filtration valves in fuel storage system
- **Response**: Municipal cleanup crews deployed over weekend

## Regulatory Context
The incident raised questions about New Jersey's data center tax abatements (PILOT agreements) versus environmental liability obligations. No wildlife impacts were officially reported, but containment costs fall to local taxpayers.

## Industry Implications
The spill underscores the need for stricter federal regulations on data center fuel systems, particularly for facilities using backup diesel generators in ecologically sensitive areas.
