### Municipal Tax Incentives for Hyperscale Data Centers

Canada's Responsible Data Centre Development Principles emphasize the need for data centres to contribute to local municipal tax bases through investments in infrastructure, jobs, and procurement. Signatories like AMD and Amazon Web Services highlight the importance of tax revenue as a measure of strategic value to Canada [^1].
