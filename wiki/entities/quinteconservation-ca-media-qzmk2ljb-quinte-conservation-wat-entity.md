Quinte Conservation is a regional conservation authority administering **8 watersheds** in Eastern Ontario, including the Salmon River, Napanee River, and Moira River systems. Key responsibilities include:

- Managing **45-460 meter elevation gradients** across **14 incorporated municipalities** (Trenton, Napanee, Belleville)
- Coordinating with **Highway 401 corridor** development while preserving **Lake Ontario** shoreline ecosystems
- Monitoring **38 identified water bodies** with First Nations territories
- Producing watershed maps under license from the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources

The organization's work impacts **population centers** and **municipal tax revenue** through land-use planning and environmental stewardship.
