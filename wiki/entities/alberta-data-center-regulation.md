### Alberta Data Center Regulatory Framework

Alberta's Environmental Protection Act (EPEA) currently excludes data centres from mandatory environmental assessments (EAs), though they may require licensing under the Water Act. The Wonder Valley and Synapse projects were exempt from EAs under section 44(3) of EPEA [^1]. Proponents argue for data centres to be included in EPEA activities to ensure statutory authorizations and proper reclamation of decommissioned sites.
