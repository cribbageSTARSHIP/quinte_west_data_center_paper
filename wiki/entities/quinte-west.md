### 2. Saputo Redevelopment Site (7 Riverside Drive)
- **Scope**: Heavy industrial parcel along the Trent River; proposed TrueNorth/Bell AI data center conversion (Planning File D09/T09/26).
- **Infrastructure Context**: Legacy 44 kV industrial electrical feeders from Hydro One Sidney Transformer Station (TS) and municipal water/wastewater tie-ins. Located ~1.2 km upstream of the Trenton Water Treatment Plant surface intake.
- **Source Asset**: `raw/maps/quinte_west_Saputo_data_center.svg`

### 3. Sonoco Mill Redevelopment Site (920 Trenton-Frankford Road, Glen Miller)
- **Scope**: 100-acre industrial brownfield along the Trent River; former Sonoco paperboard mill. 
- **Infrastructure Context**: Existing on-site 36 MW Hydro One substation capacity; proposed FirstBlock / Trinity Smart Farms indoor vertical farming and 18 MW compute cluster.
