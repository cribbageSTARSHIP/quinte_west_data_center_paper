# Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)

ISED is a federal department responsible for fostering innovation and economic development in Canada. It plays a key role in Bill 40, which grants the Minister of Energy and Mines authority to prioritize AI data centre projects that serve provincial economic interests. This has raised concerns about transparency and regulatory oversight in energy decisions related to data centres^[Source: Say No to AI Data Centres | https://climatefast.ca/project/2025-12/say-no-ai-data-centres].
