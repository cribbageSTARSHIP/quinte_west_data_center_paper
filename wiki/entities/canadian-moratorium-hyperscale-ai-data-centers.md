The Council of Canadians has called for a moratorium on hyperscale AI data center development until full environmental and community impacts are understood [[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres..., Page 1]]. Key concerns include:

1. **Energy Demand**: Proposed data centers require 20,000 MW of power (double Alberta's total generation) with 9,900% surge in requests since 2024 [[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres..., Page 3]].

2. **Environmental Impact**: Data centers increase natural gas consumption, emitting 25M tonnes CO2 annually and threatening Alberta's water resources [[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres..., Page 4]].

3. **Health Risks**: Natural gas combustion from power plants releases methane linked to respiratory illnesses, cardiovascular disease, and premature mortality [[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres..., Page 4]].

4. **Land Use**: Data centers displace agricultural land and natural spaces, with projects like Wonder Valley requiring 7,500 MW power plants [[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres..., Page 4]].

The proposed moratorium emphasizes the need for Environmental Impact Assessments (EIAs) for all data center projects [[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres..., Page 2]].
