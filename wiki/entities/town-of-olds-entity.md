# Town of Olds, Alberta

The Town of Olds (population ~10,000) is considering tax revenue implications from the proposed $10B Synapse data center complex powered by a 1.4GW natural gas plant [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854). Key details:

- **Tax Revenue Potential**: Estimated $19.5M/year if 15% of the $10B project value is assessable [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854)
- **Assessment Challenges**: Data centers are new inventory type with non-assessable computer equipment costing majority of project [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854)
- **Provincial Levy**: 2% tax levy effective Dec 31, 2026 may apply to new assessments [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854)
