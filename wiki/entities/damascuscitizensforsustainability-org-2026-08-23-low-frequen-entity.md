**Organization Overview:**
Damascus Citizens for Sustainability is a grassroots environmental advocacy group focusing on mitigating industrial noise pollution from data centers and energy infrastructure. Their 2026 report highlights health risks from low-frequency noise emissions exceeding WHO guidelines.

**Key Activities:**
- Campaigns for data center noise ordinances requiring dBC measurements
- Documents health impacts of infrasound from cooling systems
- Advocates for clean energy transition in data center operations
