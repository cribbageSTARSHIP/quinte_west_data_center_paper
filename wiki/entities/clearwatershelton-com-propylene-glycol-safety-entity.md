# ClearWater Industries

## Overview
ClearWater Industries provides glycol solutions for industrial, commercial, and data center applications. Key products include:
- Safe-T-Therm HD (high-performance glycol)
- Safe-T-Therm AL (aluminum corrosion protection)
- Safe-T-Therm GRAS (food-grade solution)

## Services
- Glycol concentration testing
- Volume studies for system charging
- Laboratory fluid analysis
- On-site maintenance and system optimization

## Applications
Serves industrial processing systems, commercial cooling, and data center environments. Implements NFPA-compliant antifreeze solutions for fire sprinkler systems.
