**Organization**: Environmental Health Sciences (EHS)

**Role**: Conducted a 2023 scientific review analyzing 60% of studies showing EMF effects on insects, including bees. Findings indicated altered behavior, oxidative stress, and DNA damage under lab conditions, but noted methodological inconsistencies in real-world data center impact assessments. `^[Source: Title | https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/are-data-centres-harming-bees-scientists-are-investigating-the-impact-of-electromagnetic-fields/articleshow/132415879.cms]`

**Connection to Bees**: Their review emphasized the need for long-term field studies to determine if EMFs from data centers represent an additional environmental pressure on pollinators, alongside established threats like pesticides and habitat loss. `^[Source: Title | https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/are-data-centres-harming-bees-scientists-are-investigating-the-impact-of-electromagnetic-fields/articleshow/132415879.cms]`
