# Mihta Askiy Data Center Environmental and Regulatory Status

The Mihta Askiy data center project in Alberta has bypassed the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) process, raising concerns about its water usage and ecological impact. The project requires significant water withdrawals for cooling, with potential contributions to local water scarcity in drought-prone regions. Regulatory oversight is limited, with developers vague on water management procedures. This project's power generation would use 833,000-5.6 million liters annually per MW, with proposed 20,000+ MW equivalents to 234,000-1.6 million Albertans' annual water use.
