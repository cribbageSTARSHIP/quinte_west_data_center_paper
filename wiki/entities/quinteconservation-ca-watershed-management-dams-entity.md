# Quinte Conservation

Quinte Conservation is a regional conservation authority responsible for managing natural resources and protecting watersheds in Quinte West, Ontario. The organization owns and operates 40 dams, which are part of a larger network of conservation authorities amalgamating into the Eastern Lake Ontario Regional Conservation Authority (ELORCA) effective February 1, 2027.

^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/watershed-management/dams/]

## Key Responsibilities

- **Water Management**: Managing dams for flood control, water supply, and habitat creation.
- **Dam Operations**: Adjusting water levels through stop logs, valves, and spillways to maintain ecological balance and public safety.
- **Environmental Protection**: Addressing invasive species like Water Soldier through herbicide application and management efforts.
- **Community Engagement**: Providing information and services to residents, municipal partners, and stakeholders through contact at info@quinteconservation.ca.

## Notable Dams

- **McLeod Dam**: A hydro-electric facility that powers 400 homes.
- **Napanee River Dam**: Built in 1836, now used for recreation.
- **Salmon River Dam**: Reconstructed in 2010, provides flood and ice management.
