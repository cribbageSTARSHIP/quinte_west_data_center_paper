**Geocortex Essentials** is a geospatial platform licensed by Esri® ArcGIS, used in the CLUPA application for environmental and infrastructure planning in Ontario. Key attributes include:

- **Purpose**: Provides mapping and spatial analysis tools for AI data center impact assessments
- **Licensing**: Commercial software with proprietary licensing terms
- **Hazards**: Potential system failures could disrupt emergency response mapping in rural areas
- **Dependencies**: Requires ArcGIS infrastructure for full functionality
