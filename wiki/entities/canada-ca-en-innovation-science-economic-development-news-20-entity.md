**Overview**: ISED is the Canadian government department responsible for advancing innovation, science, and economic development. It plays a key role in shaping national policies for digital infrastructure, including AI and data centre development.

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Leading the Responsible Data Centre Development Principles initiative.
- Collaborating with municipalities and industry stakeholders.
- Supporting Canada's AI strategy through infrastructure investment.

**Notable Projects**: Canada's AI for All strategy, responsible data centre framework, and partnerships with organizations like the Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM).
