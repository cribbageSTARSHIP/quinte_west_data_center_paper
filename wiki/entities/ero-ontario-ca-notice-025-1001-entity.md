# Ministry of Energy and Mines (MEM)

The MEM introduced amendments to the Electricity Act, 1998 as part of Bill 40 to prioritize data center electricity connections. Key responsibilities include:

- **Regulatory Framework**: Proposing regulations to set approval processes for data center grid connections^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001]
- **Economic Development**: Focusing on data centers that create high-quality jobs and strengthen Ontario's digital economy^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001]
- **Consultation Process**: Managing public input on data center connection criteria and approval timelines^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001]
