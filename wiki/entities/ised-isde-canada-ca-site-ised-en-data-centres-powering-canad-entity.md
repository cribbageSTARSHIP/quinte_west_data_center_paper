# Government of Canada - Data Centre Policy Framework

The Government of Canada established the **Responsible Data Centre Development Principles** to guide the growth of data centre infrastructure while ensuring:

- **Local Community Benefits**: Mandatory engagement with host communities and Indigenous rights holders to identify job creation, skills development, and infrastructure investment opportunities [[1]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

- **Grid Responsibility**: Proponents must fund new generation, transmission, and grid upgrades directly attributable to their projects [[2]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

- **Environmental Accountability**: Prioritization of closed-loop water cooling, waste heat recovery, and low-emission energy sources [[3]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

The framework has been endorsed by 45+ signatories including AMD, AWS, and Microsoft, aiming to position Canada as a global leader in sustainable AI infrastructure development [[4]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).
