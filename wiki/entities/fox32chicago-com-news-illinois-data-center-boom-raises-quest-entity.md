# Naaman Gambill - Chief Beekeeper, The Hive: Chicago's Beekeeping Supply Store

Naaman Gambill is the chief beekeeper for The Hive: Chicago's Beekeeping Supply Store, managing over 300 hives in the Chicagoland area. He has expressed concerns about potential impacts of data center EMFs on honeybee colonies:

> "Early results showed that these low frequencies do have a negative effect on these bees being able to learn where to go to find forage, and that could ultimately lead to negative impacts for the hives themselves." [[concepts/fox32chicago-com-news-illinois-data-center-boom-raises-quest-concept.md]]

Gambill also notes that data centers contribute to environmental stressors beyond EMFs, including:
- Water usage
- Carbon emissions
- Weather vulnerability for rooftop hives
