**InsideClimate News** is an independent, nonpartisan news organization focused on climate change and energy issues in North America. Founded in 2009, the organization has published investigative reports on the environmental and public health impacts of data center infrastructure, including diesel generator noise pollution and emissions.

Key areas of focus include:
- **Noise pollution** from data center backup generators
- **Pollutant emissions** (NOx, PM2.5) and their health impacts
- **Regulatory compliance** and industry standards for data center operations
