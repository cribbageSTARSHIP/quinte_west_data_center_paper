# Southeastern Ontario Tourism Entity

Southeastern Ontario Tourism promotes regional cycling routes like the Bay of Quinte West itinerary, highlighting:

- **Infrastructure**: Development and maintenance of cycling trails along the Trent-Severn Waterway
- **Cultural Assets**: Promotion of local attractions including craft breweries and historical waterways
- **Regional Connectivity**: Integration of Quinte West and Belleville routes with Prince Edward County

This organization curates itineraries that combine natural landscapes with urban exploration, supporting both recreational and economic development in the region. ^[Source: Title | https://www.southeasternontario.ca/itineraries/bay-of-quinte-west/]
