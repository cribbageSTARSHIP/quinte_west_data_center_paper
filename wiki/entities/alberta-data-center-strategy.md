# Alberta Data Center Strategy

The 2024 Alberta AI Data Center Strategy outlines three pillars: power capacity, sustainable cooling, and economic growth. Key regulatory triggers include:

- 1,200 MW grid allocation cap for data centers
- EPEA Section 109 restrictions on substance releases
- TIER compliance requirements for facilities emitting ≥100,000 CO2e/
