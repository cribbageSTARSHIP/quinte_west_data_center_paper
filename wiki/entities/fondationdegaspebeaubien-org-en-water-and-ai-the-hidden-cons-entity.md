# Fondation de Gaspe Beaubien

A Canadian foundation focused on environmental sustainability and digital infrastructure impacts. Their 2025 report **"Water and AI: The Hidden Constraint Behind Canada's Digital Future"** analyzes water usage patterns in AI data centers, highlighting:

- 69.54 billion liters of water used by Canadian data centers in 2026 ([Source: https://www.fondationdegaspebeaubien.org/en/water-and-ai-the-hidden-constraint-behind-canadas-digital-future/])
- 80-90% evaporative water loss in cooling systems ([Source: https://www.fondationdegaspebeaubien.org/en/water-and-ai-the-hidden-constraint-behind-canadas-digital-future/])
- Equitable water access challenges for Indigenous communities ([Source: https://www.fondationdegaspebeaubien.org/en/water-and-ai-the-hidden-constraint-behind-canadas-digital-future/])
