# ESRI Inc.

## Overview
ESRI (Environmental Systems Research Institute) is a global leader in **geospatial analysis software** and **GIS technology**. Founded in 1969, the company provides tools for:
- Spatial analysis
- Mapping and visualization
- Urban planning
- Environmental impact assessment

## Key Products
1. **ArcGIS**: Industry-leading geographic information system software
2. **GIS Proximity Analysis Tools**: Used in this study to assess data center impacts
3. **Property Valuation Modules**: Integrated with municipal assessment systems

## Role in Data Center Analysis
ESRI's GIS platforms enable:
- Spatial proximity analysis of data centers and residential zones
- Comparative sales analysis with distance band controls
- Visualization of market trends with property type filters
