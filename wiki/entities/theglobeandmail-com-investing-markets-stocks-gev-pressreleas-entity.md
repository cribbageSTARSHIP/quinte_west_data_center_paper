GE Vernova (NYSE: GEV) is a leading industrial manufacturer with a 100-gigawatt gas-turbine backlog through 2030, enabling data center operators to bypass traditional utility grids. In partnership with Chevron, GE Vernova aims to deliver 4 gigawatts of co-located gas plant power by 2027. The company's turbine pricing is projected to rise to $600 per kilowatt by 2027, a 200% increase from 2019 levels. Eaton (NYSE: ETN), a power management leader, saw data center orders spike 200% in late 2025, resulting in a record $19.6 billion electrical sector backlog by early 2026. Eaton is transitioning to a pure-play on electrification, spinning off its Mobility unit in Q1 2027 and acquiring Boyd Thermal to dominate high-margin liquid cooling.
