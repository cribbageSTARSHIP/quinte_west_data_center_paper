## AI Data Center Impact Metrics

This entity page documents consumption patterns of AI data centers:

^[Source: AI Data Centers Impact on Electric Bills, Water and More | https://www.consumerreports.org/data-centers/ai-data-centers-impact-on-electric-bills-water-and-more-a1040338678/]

## Facts:
- 1 AI data center = 250 million kWh/year electricity use
- 80% of energy goes to computing/cooling systems
- 30% higher water consumption than traditional data centers
- 90% of U.S. AI data centers use non-renewable energy sources
