# Elexicon Energy Substation Management

^[Source: https://www.quintenews.com/2022/01/25/power-outages-what-happened/]

Elexicon Energy, a key regional electricity distributor, manages the Harder Drive substation in Quinte West. During the 2022 outage event:

- Isolated the affected Harder Drive substation following a transformer failure at Hydro One [[concepts/quintenews-com-2022-01-25-power-outages-what-happened-concept.md]]
- Restored power to urban areas by 21:00 EST through staged resupply protocols
- Maintained system stability by preventing sudden load surges during recovery

The incident exposed gaps in substation redundancy and emergency response protocols, aligning with findings in the Ontario Spill Reporting Process [[concepts/ontario-ca-page-report-pollution-and-spills-concept.md]].
