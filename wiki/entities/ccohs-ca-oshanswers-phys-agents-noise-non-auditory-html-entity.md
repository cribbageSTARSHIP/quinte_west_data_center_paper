# Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety (CCOHS)

## Overview
Government of Canada agency focused on occupational health and safety, providing evidence-based information on workplace hazards including noise exposure.

## Key Contributions
- Published comprehensive guidance on non-auditory effects of noise in workplaces
- Maintained authoritative resource on noise control and occupational safety
- Provided research on noise impact thresholds and communication degradation
- Offered practical guidelines for noise management in industrial settings
