
# oe clean energy resources meet data center electricity demand entity

## Data Center Electricity Demand

This entity page documents energy consumption patterns of data centers:

^[Source: Clean Energy Resources to Meet Data Center Electricity Demand | https://www.energy.gov/oe/clean-energy-resources-meet-data-center-electricity-demand]

## Facts:
- 2% of U.S. electricity goes to data centers
- 40% of data center energy is used for cooling
- 80% of data centers use fossil fuels for power
