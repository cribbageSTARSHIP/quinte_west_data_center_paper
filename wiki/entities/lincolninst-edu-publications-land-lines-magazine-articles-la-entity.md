# Lincoln Institute of Land Policy

The Lincoln Institute of Land Policy researches land use and environmental impacts of infrastructure, including AI data centers. Key findings include:
- Data centers consume 500,000 gallons/day (10% of Newton County, GA's total water use)
- 80% of UK data centers located in water-stressed London area
- Advocates for integrated land/water/energy planning

^[Source: Data Drain: The Land and Water Impacts of the AI Boom | https://www.lincolninst.edu/publications/land-lines-magazine/articles/land-water-impacts-data-centers/]
