# Quinte West Water Sewer FAQ

## Organization Overview

Quinte West is a municipality in Ontario, Canada, known for its focus on sustainable water and sewer management practices. The FAQ document provides detailed information on the municipality's approach to water conservation, sewer system maintenance, and environmental stewardship.

## Key Contact Information

- **Website**: [Quinte West Official Site](https://quintewest.ca)
- **Water Department**: [Water Services Page](https://quintewest.ca/water-environment)
- **Environmental Policies**: [Sustainability Initiatives](https://quintewest.ca/sustainability)

## Notable Projects

- **Green Infrastructure Program**: Aims to reduce stormwater runoff through permeable pavement and rain garden installations.
- **Sewer Overflow Prevention**: Implementation of real-time monitoring systems to detect and mitigate potential overflow events.
