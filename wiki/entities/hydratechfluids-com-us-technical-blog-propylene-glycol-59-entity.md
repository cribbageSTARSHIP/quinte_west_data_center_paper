# Hydratech Fluids Corporation

Hydratech Fluids is a global provider of high-performance glycol and antifreeze solutions for industrial, commercial, and food processing applications. Their products are formulated to maximize coefficient of performance (CoP) through advanced fluid dynamics and thermodynamics research.

The company produces Propylene Glycol-based heat transfer fluids for both cooling and heating systems, with formulations meeting USP, EP, and JP pharmacopeia standards. They also offer a vegetable-based Propylene Glycol variant with improved environmental profiles.

Key technical services include:
- Fluid Monitoring Program (FMP) for closed-loop system maintenance
- SureFlow program for biological contamination prevention
- Custom formulation services for specific temperature ranges and applications
