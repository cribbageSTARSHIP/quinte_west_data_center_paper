Quinte Conservation is a regional conservation authority responsible for managing and protecting conservation areas within the Quinte watershed in Ontario, Canada. These areas are established to protect local ecosystems and support recreational use, including activities like hiking, birdwatching, and picnicking. Quinte Conservation oversees over 30,000 acres of conservation lands across the 6,000 km² watershed, offering public access to 75 km of nature trails. Additionally, Quinte Conservation has partnered with Paddle Pod Canada to introduce self-serve kayak rentals at Depot Lakes and Massassauga Point Conservation Areas, providing affordable, on-demand access to kayaking from late June through late September.^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/outdoor-spaces/areas/]

Quinte Conservation is preparing to merge into the Eastern Lake Ontario Regional Conservation Authority (ELORCA) as part of changes to the Conservation Authorities Act, effective February 1, 2027. This transition is part of the amalgamation of Ontario's 36 conservation authorities into 9 Regional Conservation Authorities. Despite this transition, Quinte Conservation will continue to provide and carry out all of its programs with the high level of service expected by residents, municipal partners, and stakeholders.^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/outdoor-spaces/areas/]
