# National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI)

The NCBI, a division of the National Library of Medicine (NLM), operates under the U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH). As a [[concepts/01-power-grid.md]](hyperscale AI data center)^[Source: Title | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK598037/], the NCBI manages large-scale genomic and biomedical data repositories requiring significant electrical power and water cooling resources.

Key operational metrics include: [[concepts/02-water-ecology.md]](evaporative cooling losses)^[Source: Title | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK598037/] of 12-18 million gallons annually, and [[concepts/01-power-grid.md]](peak power demand)^[Source: Title | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK598037/] exceeding 40 MW during genomic sequencing operations.
