# Tax Foundation

The Tax Foundation is a nonpartisan research organization based in Washington, DC, focused on tax policy analysis. Their 2026 study on data center taxation examines how state and local tax policies influence data center location decisions across 12 U.S. jurisdictions.

**Key Activities**:
- Analyzing sales, property, and corporate tax impacts on data centers
- Comparing tax burdens in Phoenix, AZ; Santa Clara County, CA; Charlotte, NC; and other locations
- Advocating for neutral tax treatment of data centers
