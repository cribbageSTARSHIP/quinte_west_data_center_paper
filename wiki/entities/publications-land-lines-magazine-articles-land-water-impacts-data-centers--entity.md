## Data Center Water Use

This entity page documents the water consumption patterns of data centers:

^[Source: Land, Water, and Data Centers | https://www.lincolninst.edu/publications/land-lines-magazine/articles/land-water-impacts-data-centers/]

## Facts:
- Typical data center uses 3-5 million gallons of water daily
- Cooling systems account for 30-50% of total water usage
- Regions with limited water access face greater operational challenges
