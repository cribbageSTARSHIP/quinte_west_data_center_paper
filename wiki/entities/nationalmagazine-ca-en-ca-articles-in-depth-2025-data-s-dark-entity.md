Canada's largest data center operator, eStruxture plans a major facility near Calgary. The project faces water licensing challenges in Alberta, where new data center development requires purchasing existing water rights or securing municipal supplies. This highlights tensions between AI economic growth and water resource management. [[concepts/nationalmagazine-ca-en-ca-articles-in-depth-2025-data-s-dark-concept.md]]
