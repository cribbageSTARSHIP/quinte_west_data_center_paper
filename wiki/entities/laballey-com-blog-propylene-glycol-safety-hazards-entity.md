# Laballey

Laballey is a chemical supplier specializing in high-purity propylene glycol for pharmaceutical, food, industrial, and laboratory applications. Key details from [[raw/articles/laballey-com-blog-propylene-glycol-safety-hazards.md]]:

- Provides USP/NF pharmaceutical grade, FCC food grade, and technical grades of propylene glycol
- Offers Safety Data Sheets (SDS) and Certificates of Analysis (COA) for each production lot
- Supports applications in drug manufacturing, food processing, aircraft deicing, and industrial cooling systems
- Maintains compliance with FDA GRAS standards, USP/NF monographs, and EPA regulations
- Operates a Resource Library with technical documents and application guides
