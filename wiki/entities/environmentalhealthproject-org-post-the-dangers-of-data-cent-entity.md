# Environmental Health Project

The Environmental Health Project is a non-profit organization focused on mitigating the environmental and public health impacts of industrial activities, including AI data centers. Key initiatives include:

- Advocating for renewable energy adoption in data center operations
- Researching the health effects of noise and light pollution from data centers
- Publishing analyses on water usage and pollution risks associated with hyperscale data centers
- Supporting community efforts to regulate data center expansion
- Documenting the disproportionate impact of data centers on minority and low-income communities

The organization's work is cited in multiple studies on data center environmental impacts [[Source: Title | https://www.environmentalhealthproject.org/post/the-dangers-of-data-centers]].
