## Quinte West Data Center Entity

The Trenton Service Area data center operations are managed by **Quinte West**, a regional energy authority responsible for power grid management and infrastructure development in Eastern Ontario.

Key operational metrics from the 2021 Annual Report:
- **12.4 million liters/year** of municipal water used for evaporative cooling [^[Source: Title | https://quintewest.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/2021_Annual_Report_-_Trenton_Service_Area.pdf]]
- **8.3%** of municipal industrial water allocation
- **42°C** thermal discharge temperatures during peak cooling cycles
- **15°C** above ambient river temperatures
