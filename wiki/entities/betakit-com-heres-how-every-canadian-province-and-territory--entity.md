# BetaKit Data Centre Policy Analysis

## Organization Overview
BetaKit is a Canadian media outlet that published an in-depth analysis of provincial data centre governance frameworks ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/]. The article details how provinces like Alberta, Ontario, and Saskatchewan are attracting hyperscale facilities through targeted policies, while others like Manitoba are actively disincentivizing development.

## Key Activities
- **Policy Documentation**: The article categorizes provincial approaches into four frameworks, analyzing economic incentives, environmental regulations, and grid reliability measures ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
- **Stakeholder Engagement**: BetaKit highlights requirements for municipal, Indigenous, and utility stakeholder participation in approvals ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
- **Comparative Analysis**: The analysis contrasts Alberta's 2% data centre levy with Québec's 13¢/kWh tariffs, noting legal challenges to these measures ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
