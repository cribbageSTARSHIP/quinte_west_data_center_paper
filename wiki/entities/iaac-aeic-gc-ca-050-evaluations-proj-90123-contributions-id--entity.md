# Beacon AI Centers Heartland Project

## Overview
The Beacon AI Centers Heartland Project is a proposed hyperscale data center initiative facing significant community opposition due to its environmental and socioeconomic impacts [[wikilinks]][concepts/iaac-aeic-gc-ca-050-evaluations-proj-90123-contributions-id--concept.md].

## Key Impacts
1. **Water Usage**: Requires 1500 cubic meters/day, straining local water resources during droughts [[wikilinks]][concepts/canada-ca-en-health-canada-services-publications-healthy-liv-concept.md].
2. **Energy Demand**: Exacerbates power grid strain and increases utility costs for residents [[wikilinks]][concepts/responsible-data-centre-development.md].
3. **Community Displacement**: Threatens housing and recreational land use for long-term community benefit [[wikilinks]][entities/climatefast-ca-project-2025-12-say-no-ai-data-centres-entity.md].
4. **Environmental Harm**: Risks wildlife habitat disruption and pollution [[wikilinks]][concepts/air-pollutant-emissions-in-canada.md].
