# MLTAikins - AI Data Centre Legal Consulting

MLTAikins is a Canadian law firm specializing in legal and regulatory guidance for AI data centre development. Their services include:

- Corporate structuring for data centre projects
- Tax incentive optimization for clean energy components
- Compliance with provincial energy market regulations
- Environmental permitting navigation
- Stakeholder engagement strategies
- Indigenous consultation frameworks

The firm's insights highlight the importance of:
- Separating generation and data centre entities for regulatory compliance
- Engaging with provincial crown utilities for power supply
- Conducting thorough environmental assessments
- Implementing community benefits plans
