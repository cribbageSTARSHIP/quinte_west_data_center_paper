DataOne Inc. is a data center operator in New Jersey linked to Microsoft. The company was fined $1m for operating 62 unpermitted gas-fired generators emitting large amounts of NOx, a dangerous group of smog-producing gases linked to asthma, heart attacks, and premature death [[concepts/ecology-wa-gov-air-climate-air-quality-data-centers-concept.md]].

The Vineland facility's generators operated for over 46,000 hours before state regulators intervened. Analysis estimates annual NOx emissions of 140 tons, with potential emissions of 1,500 tons/year at full capacity ^[Source: Title | https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/01/us-datacenter-new-jersey-pollution].

DataOne disputes the need for permits and plans to apply for them while continuing operations. The company claims to be transitioning to low-emission fuel cells as part of its permanent power solution ^[Source: Title | https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/01/us-datacenter-new-jersey-pollution].
