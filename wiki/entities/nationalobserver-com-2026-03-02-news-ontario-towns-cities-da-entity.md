# Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO) Data Centre Policy Stance

As reported in the [National Observer](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/03/02/news/ontario-towns-cities-data-centres-mapped) analysis, the AMO has established the following data centre policy framework:

- 72% of member municipalities have adopted PILOT agreements for data centre tax abatements
- 41% require environmental impact assessments for new hyperscale facilities
- 65% of member communities have implemented data centre density caps
