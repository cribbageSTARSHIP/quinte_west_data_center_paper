# Acoustical Consultants Inc.

## Overview
Acoustical Consultants Inc. is a leading environmental acoustics firm specializing in noise measurement, impact assessment, and regulatory compliance consulting. The organization provides technical expertise on both dBA and dBC measurement standards for:

- Industrial noise control
- Urban development acoustic planning
- Transportation infrastructure noise modeling
- Noise nuisance litigation support

## Key Services

- **Noise Impact Assessments**: Using both dBA and dBC measurements to create comprehensive noise profiles.
- **Regulatory Compliance Audits**: Ensuring adherence to federal, state, and local noise ordinances.
- **Noise Modeling and Prediction**: Simulating noise propagation using advanced acoustic software.
- **Community Noise Complaint Resolution**: Investigating and mitigating noise disturbances.

## Industry Involvement

The firm contributes to national acoustic standards development and participates in research initiatives related to low-frequency noise measurement techniques.
