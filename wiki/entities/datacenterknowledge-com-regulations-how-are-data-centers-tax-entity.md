The Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO) represents over 200 municipalities in advocating for data center taxation policies. Key activities include:

- Negotiating PILOT agreements with major data center operators
- Developing model bylaws for tax revenue sharing
- Providing technical assistance to municipalities on data center tax implications
