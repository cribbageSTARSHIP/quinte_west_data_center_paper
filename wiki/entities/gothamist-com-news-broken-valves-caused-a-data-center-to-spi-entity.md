# Meadowlands Data Center Spill Incident

## Incident Overview

On [insert date], a 5,000-gallon diesel spill occurred at a data center in New Jersey's Meadowlands due to broken valves. The incident triggered EPA investigations and highlighted gaps in fuel system containment protocols for hyperscale facilities.

## Regulatory Context

- **EPA SPCC Rules**: Require facilities storing >1,000 gallons of oil to have secondary containment and spill response plans.
- **NJDEP Requirements**: Mandate emergency action plans for facilities in environmentally sensitive areas.

## Technical Details

- **Fuel System Configuration**: The facility used underground storage tanks connected to backup generators.
- **Failure Mode**: Corrosion-induced valve failure in the fuel transfer system.
- **Containment Gaps**: Lack of secondary containment ponds and inadequate spill detection sensors.
