# The Noise Chap

**The Noise Chap** is a resource for noise safety information, focusing on explaining decibel (dB) measurements, regulatory thresholds, and hearing protection. Their content is widely referenced in noise assessments and occupational safety guidelines.

## Key Contributions

- Clarifies the difference between **dB(A)** (average noise exposure) and **dB(C)** (peak impact noise).
- Provides practical examples of noise scenarios (e.g., warehouse impacts, firearms use).
- Explains how to calculate hearing protector effectiveness using **SNR** ratings.
