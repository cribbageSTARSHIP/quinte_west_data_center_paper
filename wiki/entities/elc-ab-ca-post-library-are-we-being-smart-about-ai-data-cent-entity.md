# Environmental Law Centre (ELC) - Alberta

The Environmental Law Centre (ELC) is Alberta's leading environmental charity, specializing in legal advocacy for sustainable data center development. Founded in 1986, the ELC has been instrumental in:

1. **Challenging Weak Regulatory Frameworks**: Highlighting gaps in environmental assessments for data centers (see [[concepts/elc-ab-ca-post-library-are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-cent-concept.md]]).
2. **Community Engagement**: Supporting local opposition to projects like the Wonder Valley AI data center through legal analysis and public education (^[Source: https://elc.ab.ca/post-library/are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-centres/]).
3. **Policy Reform Advocacy**: Pushing for statutory changes to include data centers in the Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act's Schedule of Activities.

The ELC's 2026 report on Alberta's data center regulatory landscape has become a critical reference for municipalities and developers (^[Source: https://elc.ab.ca/post-library/are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-centres/]).
