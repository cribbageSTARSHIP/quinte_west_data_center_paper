# Equinix Data Center Incident

The Equinix data center in Secaucus, New Jersey, experienced a significant diesel spill on September 11, 2026, due to a computer error. This incident resulted in the release of approximately 5,500 gallons of diesel fuel and waste into a stream of the Hackensack River.^[Source: Title | https://thestute.com/2026/10/02/thousands-of-gallons-of-diesel-spill-into-secaucus-creek-from-data-center/]

The spill has raised concerns about the environmental impact of data centers and the need for stricter regulations to prevent such incidents. The incident also highlights the potential for data centers to cause significant harm to local ecosystems and communities.
