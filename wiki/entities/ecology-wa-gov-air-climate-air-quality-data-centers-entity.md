The Washington State Department of Ecology regulates air quality permitting for data centers, focusing on diesel exhaust and nitrogen dioxide emissions from backup generators. Key responsibilities include:
- Issuing air quality permits with emission limits
- Conducting health risk assessments for new data center projects
- Monitoring compliance with NO2 and PM2.5 standards
- Enforcing regulations in Quincy, Wenatchee, and other data center hubs
