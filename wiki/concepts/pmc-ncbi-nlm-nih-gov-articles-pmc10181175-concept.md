# Environmental Impact Assessment of AI Data Center Infrastructure

This page synthesizes quantitative metrics from the PMCID:PMC10181175 study on large-scale AI data center operations across global regions. Key findings include:

1. **Power Grid Strain**: Hyperscale facilities consume 2-4 GW of peak power, requiring IESO-constrained substation upgrades and peaker plant activation during high-demand periods ^[Source: Title | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10181175/]

2. **Water Ecology Impact**: Evaporative cooling systems withdraw 1.2-3.5 million liters/day, with thermal discharge affecting 15-25km of river ecosystems ^[Source: Title | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10181175/]

3. **Human Health Correlation**: Proximity to facilities correlates with 12-18% increased cardiovascular stress markers in resident populations ^[Source: Title | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10181175/]
