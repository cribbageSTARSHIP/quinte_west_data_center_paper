# Environmental Impact of AI Data Centres

AI data centres contribute significantly to environmental degradation through:

1. **Greenhouse Gas Emissions**: Reliance on fossil fuels for energy increases GHG emissions^[Source: Say No to AI Data Centres | https://climatefast.ca/project/2025-12/say-no-ai-data-centres].
2. **Water Consumption**: Massive water usage leads to ecological disruption and biodiversity loss^[Source: Say No to AI Data Centres | https://climatefast.ca/project/2025-12/say-no-ai-data-centres].
3. **Economic Disparities**: Benefits accrue to American tech firms while local communities bear environmental and health costs^[Source: Say No to AI Data Centres | https://climatefast.ca/project/2025-12/say-no-ai-data-centres].
