A 2024 Virginia legislative report found that data center growth could increase typical residential electricity bills by $14-$37 per month by 2040, representing a 9-25% increase over current averages^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger their local communities and the country as a whole.pdf, Page 7]. These rising costs disproportionately affect lower-income households, who spend a larger share of their budgets on electricity and face increased risks of heat-related illness and cold-related cardiovascular stress when bills rise^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger their local communities and the country as a whole.pdf, Page 7].
