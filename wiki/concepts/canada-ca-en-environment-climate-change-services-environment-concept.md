Canada's air pollutant emissions tracking program monitors sulphur oxides (SOX), nitrogen oxides (NOX), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), carbon monoxide (CO), ammonia (NH3), fine particulate matter (PM2.5), and black carbon emissions [[wikilinks]][[wikilinks]]. Key findings include:

- 2024 emissions of 5 major pollutants were 81% lower than 1990 levels [[wikilinks]] ^[Source: Title | https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/environmental-indicators/air-pollutant-emissions.html]
- Transportation and industrial activities remain primary emission sources ^[Source: Title | https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/environmental-indicators/air-pollutant-emissions.html]
- Emissions reductions correlate with national air quality improvements ^[Source: Title | https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/environmental-indicators/air-pollutant-emissions.html]
