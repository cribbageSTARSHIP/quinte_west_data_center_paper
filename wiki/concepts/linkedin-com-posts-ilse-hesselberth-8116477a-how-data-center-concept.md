# Data Center Fuel Spill Containment Mechanisms

## Technical Overview
Data center fuel containment systems must address both acute spills (e.g., 3,500 gallons of diesel released into Hackensack River tributaries) and chronic leaks from backup generators. [[concepts/patch-com-new-jersey-secaucus-data-center-spills-5-500-gallo-concept.md]]

## Regulatory Context
New Jersey requires data centers to maintain secondary containment barriers for fuel storage tanks. The Secaucus facility's failed filtration valves (^[Source: Title | https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ilse-hesselberth-8116477a_how-data-centers-destroy-humanity-and-all-activity-7506333573877264386-2e5C]) highlight gaps in municipal oversight of industrial fuel systems.

## Environmental Impact
Fuel spills create three-tiered risks: immediate aquatic toxicity, long-term soil contamination, and indirect climate impacts from emergency response operations. [[entities/patch-com-new-jersey-secaucus-data-center-spills-5-500-gallo-entity.md]]
