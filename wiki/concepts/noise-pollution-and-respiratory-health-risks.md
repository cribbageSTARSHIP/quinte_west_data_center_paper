# Noise Pollution and Respiratory Health Risks

Data centers generate continuous noise from cooling systems and generators, creating environmental stressors. This persistent noise exposure has been linked to sleep disturbance, cardiovascular issues, and cognitive impacts. Studies show that noise levels near data centers in Virginia range between 40 and 59 decibels, approaching or exceeding recommended limits. Long-term exposure is associated with heart disease, hypertension, and mental health concerns. Children exposed to noise pollution exhibit poor school performance and increased stress. The constant noise from 24/7 data center operations could exacerbate these risks, impacting long-term health outcomes.
