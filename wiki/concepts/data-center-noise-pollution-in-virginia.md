Virginia's rapid data center expansion has led to significant noise pollution concerns. Residents in Loudoun County report an 'eerie hum' from facilities like True North, a data center operated by Compass Datacenters [^[Source: PDF HOME TECH Virginia's 'Data Center Alley' residents say an eerie hum is ... | https://www.pwcva.gov/assets/2024-09/Data-Center+Boom+Leads+to+Noise+Disruptions+in+Loudoun+County,+Virginia+-+2.pdf]]. The state's Data Center Accountability Framework mandates cumulative impact analyses for diesel generators and requires facilities to adopt cleaner backup power solutions [^[Source: Data Center Accountability Framework - energychief.virginia.gov | https://www.energychief.virginia.gov/datacenters/]].
