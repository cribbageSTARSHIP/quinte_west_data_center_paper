This page synthesizes key findings from the *Globe and Mail* article on the backlash against AI data center development in Canada, focusing on community concerns, environmental impacts, and policy recommendations.

### Core Concerns
1. **Community Backlash**: 68% of Canadians oppose large AI data centers near their homes due to fears of:
   - Strained electrical grids and rising electricity rates
   - Water resource depletion from evaporative cooling
   - Noise pollution and potential property value declines
   - Perceived concentration of wealth among tech billionaires

2. **Environmental Impacts**:
   - GHG emissions from backup diesel generators
   - Thermal discharge from cooling systems
   - Aquifer depletion in regions with limited water resources

### Policy Recommendations
#### For Federal/Provincial Governments
- Ensure grid infrastructure can handle additional loads without rate hikes
- Mandate sustainable water management practices
- Support local job creation and Canadian innovation (e.g., Extract Energy, Cura Climate)
- Require transparent reporting of energy/water usage metrics

#### For Municipalities
- Negotiate financial participation in data center benefits (e.g., tax abatements with community reinvestment)
- Create apprenticeship programs for local talent development
- Require noise mitigation measures

#### For Developers
- Pay full costs of construction/operation
- Invest in grid upgrades that benefit residents
- Partner with local startups to leverage data center capabilities
