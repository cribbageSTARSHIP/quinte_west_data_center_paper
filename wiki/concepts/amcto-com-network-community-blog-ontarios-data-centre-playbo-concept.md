# Ontario Data Centre Playbook for Municipal Governance

^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-need-know]

Ontario's proposed Data Centre Playbook introduces a provincial framework to guide data centre projects' evaluation and approval for grid connection. The framework focuses on three pillars:

1. **Economic Development**: Balancing capital investment with community impacts.
2. **Data Security & Digital Sovereignty**: Ensuring provincial oversight of grid access.
3. **Community Benefits**: Defining "significant and meaningful" benefits for host municipalities (^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-need-know]).

Key municipal responses include:
- Mississauga's one-year moratorium on AI data centres.
- Toronto's study on land-use compatibility and environmental impacts (^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-need-know]).

The Playbook prioritizes closed-loop cooling systems to reduce water consumption, addressing municipal concerns about evaporative cooling losses (^[Source: https://www.amcto.com/network-community/blog/ontarios-data-centre-playbook-what-municipalities-need-know]).
