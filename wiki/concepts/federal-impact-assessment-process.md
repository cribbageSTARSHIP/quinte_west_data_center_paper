The federal impact assessment process under the Impact Assessment Act (IAA) requires a federal IA if a project falls into the list in the Physical Activities Regulations, including large fossil-fuel and hydro-electric power plants. Key criteria include impacts on federal jurisdiction areas like fish habitats, species at risk, and Indigenous peoples. The Minister may designate projects for IA if there are adverse effects within federal jurisdiction. [[environmental-assessment-process]] and [[alberta-electric-system-operator-aeso]] provide additional context on provincial and Alberta-specific regulations.
