# Canadian Provincial Data Centre Policies

This concept page synthesizes the diverse approaches taken by Canadian provinces and territories in governing data centre development, as detailed in the BetaKit article ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].

## Key Policy Categories
1. **Targeted Incentives** (Alberta, Ontario, Saskatchewan, New Brunswick): These provinces offer streamlined approvals, tax breaks, and power generation mandates to attract hyperscale data centres. Alberta's "data centre concierge service" and Ontario's "white glove service" are notable examples ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
2. **No Standalone Policies** (Nova Scotia, PEI, Newfoundland): These regions lack formal frameworks due to energy capacity constraints or lack of interest, though Nova Scotia is reconsidering under federal guidelines ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
3. **Mixed Approaches** (BC, Québec): British Columbia uses competitive bidding to manage grid demand, while Québec implemented high tariffs (13¢/kWh) on large data centres, facing legal challenges ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
4. **Disincentivizing Policies** (Manitoba): Manitoba actively restricts large-scale data centres through power categorization, financial levies, and case-by-case vetoes ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].

## Cross-Cutting Themes
- **Grid Reliability**: All provinces consider electricity costs and grid capacity, with Alberta and Saskatchewan emphasizing self-supplied power ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
- **Environmental Impact**: Water use, emissions, and noise are regulated, particularly in Alberta and Nova Scotia ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
- **Community Engagement**: Ontario and New Brunswick require municipalities and Indigenous groups to participate in approvals ^[Source: BetaKit | https://betakit.com/heres-how-every-canadian-province-and-territory-is-governing-data-centres/].
