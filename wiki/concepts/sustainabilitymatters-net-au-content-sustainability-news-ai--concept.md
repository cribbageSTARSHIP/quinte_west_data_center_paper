# Environmental Impact of AI on Bees

A report by the Centre for AI, Trust and Governance at The University of Sydney highlights the potential ecological risks of AI expansion, particularly on bees. Key findings include:

- AI-driven digital infrastructure could contribute **4.8 to 15.4 gigatonnes of cumulative CO₂ emissions** globally between 2024-2035 (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
- This could drive global temperatures by **0.0026°C to 0.0084°C** (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
- Australia's bee-dependent agriculture ($4.6 billion annually) faces increased risks from heat stress, bushfires, and Varroa mites (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
- The report introduces a **bee barometer** framework to assess climate impacts on pollinators (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).

Recommendations include conditional grid access for data centers, carbon-aware computing, and cross-sector partnerships (^[Source: https://www.sustainabilitymatters.net.au/content/sustainability/news/ai-may-have-devastating-impact-on-bees-study-1075906291]).
