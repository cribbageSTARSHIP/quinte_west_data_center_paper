**Cardiovascular Impacts from Data Center Emissions and Air Pollutants**

Exposure to fine particulate matter (PM2.5) from data center emissions contributes to cardiovascular stress, increasing the risk of heart attacks and strokes. These impacts are particularly concerning for communities near data centers, where air pollution levels can be significantly higher.
