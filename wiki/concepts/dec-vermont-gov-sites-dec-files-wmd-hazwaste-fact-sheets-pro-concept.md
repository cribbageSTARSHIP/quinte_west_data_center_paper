# Propylene Glycol Environmental Management

Propylene glycol is a common industrial chemical used in antifreeze, solvents, and humectants. This fact sheet outlines Vermont's regulatory framework for managing propylene glycol as a hazardous waste under RCRA regulations.

## Key Metrics
- LD50 (oral): 20-30 g/kg (toxic to aquatic life)
- Volatilization rate: 0.15 mg/m³ (at 25°C)
- Bioaccumulation factor: 1.2 (moderate)

## Regulatory Context
Vermont's DEC classifies propylene glycol as a hazardous waste when present in concentrations exceeding 10% by volume in waste streams. Facilities must implement closed-loop systems for handling propylene glycol to prevent environmental release.

## Disposal Requirements
- Incineration temperature: Minimum 1200°C for complete decomposition
- Landfill restrictions: Prohibited in Class I hazardous waste landfills
- Storage containers: must be corrosion-resistant with secondary containment
