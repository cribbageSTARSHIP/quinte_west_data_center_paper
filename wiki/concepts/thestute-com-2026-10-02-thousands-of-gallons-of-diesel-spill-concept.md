# Data Center Diesel Spill Incident and Environmental Impact

On September 11, 2026, a computer error at the Equinix data center in Secaucus, New Jersey, resulted in the release of approximately 5,500 gallons of diesel fuel and waste into a stream of the Hackensack River.^[Source: Title | https://thestute.com/2026/10/02/thousands-of-gallons-of-diesel-spill-into-secaucus-creek-from-data-center/]

This incident highlights the environmental risks associated with data centers, particularly the potential for large-scale spills of toxic substances. The spill occurred in a small stream within the Hackensack River ecosystem, raising concerns about the impact on local wildlife and drinking water sources.^[Source: Title | https://thestute.com/2026/10/02/thousands-of-gallons-of-diesel-spill-into-secaucus-creek-from-data-center/]

The spill underscores the need for stricter regulations and safety measures in data center operations to prevent such incidents. [[concepts/ecology-wa-gov-air-climate-air-quality-data-centers-concept.md]] [[entities/equinix.md]]
