Environmental impact assessments (EIA) for data centers are critical, particularly concerning air pollution and health risks. The U.S. EPA's Clean Air Act Resources for Data Centers outlines permitting processes to address emissions from backup generators, including nitrogen dioxide (NO2) and particulate matter, which can cause respiratory issues [[wikilinks]](data-centers-air-pollution-associated-with-lung-issues). Additionally, data centers must prepare health impact assessments for toxic air pollution, such as diesel exhaust, before obtaining construction permits [[wikilinks]](diesel-pollution-from-data-centers).

A report by Community & Environmental Defense Services (CEDS) indicates that emissions from data centers can lead to breathing issues and premature deaths for nearby residents, with risks increasing near multiple facilities. This highlights the necessity for stringent EIAs to mitigate health impacts [[wikilinks]](data-centers-air-pollution-associated-with-lung-issues).
