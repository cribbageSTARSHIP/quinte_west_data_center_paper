Hyperscale data centers deploy vast arrays of multi-megawatt backup diesel generators. Operators routinely seek regulatory variances to install EPA Tier 2 generators rather than Tier 4 units due to equipment lead times and capital expenditure disparities^[Source: 03_pollutants_spills.md].

Tier 2 generators emit significantly higher concentrations of nitrogen oxides ($NO_x$) and fine particulate matter ($PM_{2.5}$)^[Source: 03_pollutants_spills.md]. During emergency switchovers, routine monthly load-bank testing, and grid curtailment intervals, localized $PM_{2.5}$ plumes can cause acute respiratory and cardiovascular hazards in nearby downwind communities^[Source: 05_human_health.md].
