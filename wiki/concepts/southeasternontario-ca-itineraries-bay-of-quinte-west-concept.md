# Bay of Quinte West Cycling Itinerary Concept

This 68km cycling route explores the historical Trent-Severn Waterway, connecting Belleville and Quinte West through rural landscapes, locks, and coastal vistas. Key features include:

- **Historic Waterway**: Follows part of the Trent-Severn Waterway with locks along the Trent River
- **Rural Vistas**: Gentle hills and farms spanning Hastings County
- **Bay Connections**: Routes from Foxboro south along the Moira River to the Bay of Quinte
- **Local Culture**: Craft brewery stop near Belleville (10km from finish)
- **Urban Exploration**: Front Street shops and shoreline parks in Belleville

This moderate-to-challenging route can be combined with the Bay of Quinte – East itinerary for multi-day adventures. ^[Source: Title | https://www.southeasternontario.ca/itineraries/bay-of-quinte-west/]
