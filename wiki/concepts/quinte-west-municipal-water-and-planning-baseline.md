# Quinte West Municipal Water Infrastructure & Planning Baseline

## Water System Capacity
- **216 km** of water mains^[Source: Quinte West Water and Sewer FAQ | https://quintewest.ca/water-environment/water-sewer-faq/]
- **132 km** of sanitary sewer lines^[Source: Quinte West Water and Sewer FAQ | https://quintewest.ca/water-environment/water-sewer-faq/]
- **8,800** customer connections to water/sewer systems^[Source: Quinte West Water and Sewer FAQ | https://quintewest.ca/water-environment/water-sewer-faq/]
- **6,000 megalitres** of wastewater treated annually^[Source: Quinte West Water and Sewer FAQ | https://quintewest.ca/water-environment/water-sewer-faq/]
- **7,000 megalitres** of drinking water treated annually^[Source: Quinte West Water and Sewer FAQ | https://quintewest.ca/water-environment/water-sewer-faq/]

## Planning & Zoning Activity
- **7 active zoning bylaw amendment proposals** as of 2026-10-03^[Source: Quinte West Legislative Notices | https://quintewest.ca/news-and-notices/legislative-notices/]
- **3 proposed condominium developments** under review^[Source: Quinte West Legislative Notices | https://quintewest.ca/news-and-notices/legislative-notices/]
- **Vacant property amendments** and **heritage property evaluations** ongoing^[Source: Quinte West Planning and Development | https://quintewest.ca/planning-development-business/]
