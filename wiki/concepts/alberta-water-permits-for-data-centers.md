Alberta requires water permits for data centre operations under the Water Act, specifically for activities altering water flow, levels, or aquatic environments (section 1(1)(b)). The Wonder Valley AI Data Centre example highlights the need for water licences, with a preliminary certificate granted for 24 million cubic metres annual usage. Section 619 of the MGA allows AUC approvals to supersede municipal bylaws.
