Data centers generate persistent noise from cooling systems and backup generators, with decibel levels ranging between 40-59 dB [^[Source: raw/papers/04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md, Page 1473]]. This exceeds the EPA's recommended 55 dBA outdoor limit (day-night average) [^[Source: raw/papers/04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md, Page 1478]]. Continuous exposure to such noise levels is associated with cardiovascular risks, sleep disturbances, and cognitive impairment in children [^[Source: raw/papers/04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md, Page 1480]].
