# Canadian Grid Integration and Demand Flexibility

## Key Insights
- **Rapid AI-driven data centre growth** is straining electricity grids, with new facilities demanding **100+ MW** of power (equivalent to **350,000 EVs annually**)
  ^[Source: Canadian Climate Institute | https://climateinstitute.ca/smart-way-integrate-artificial-intelligence-data-centres-canada-electricity-grids/]
- **Quebec's hydro system** faces pressure from data centres, with **10 GW** of proposed projects in interconnection queues
  ^[Source: Canadian Climate Institute | https://climateinstitute.ca/smart-way-integrate-artificial-intelligence-data-centres-canada-electricity-grids/]
- **Alberta's gas-dependent plans** could double electricity emissions if data centres rely on fossil fuels
  ^[Source: Canadian Climate Institute | https://climateinstitute.ca/smart-way-integrate-artificial-intelligence-data-centres-canada-electricity-grids/]

## Policy Challenges
- Grid operators in **Ontario** and **B.C.** lack frameworks to prioritize data centre connections
  ^[Source: Canadian Climate Institute | https://climateinstitute.ca/smart-way-integrate-artificial-intelligence-data-centres-canada-electricity-grids/]
- **Quebec's 2023 framework** has **blocked all new data centres** due to grid constraints
  ^[Source: Canadian Climate Institute | https://climateinstitute.ca/smart-way-integrate-artificial-intelligence-data-centres-canada-electricity-grids/]
