# Data Center Proximity and Property Valuation Analysis

## Key Findings
Data centers can have **mixed impacts** on nearby property values depending on:
1. Distance from residential zones (`[[concepts/06-municipal-tax-revenue-concept.md]]`)
2. Local economic context (`[[entities/association-of-municipalities-of-ontario-entity.md]]`)
3. Perceived aesthetics vs actual infrastructure benefits

## GIS Analysis Methodology
1. Create distance bands around facilities
2. Compare sale prices before/after development
3. Control for property type, size, and other market factors

## Case Study Insights
- **Negative impacts** often observed when:
  - Data centers are within 1km of residential areas
  - In communities without prior industrial infrastructure
- **Positive impacts** seen in:
  - Established tech hubs with existing infrastructure
  - Communities seeking economic diversification (`[[entities/union-of-bc-municipalities-entity.md]]`)
