This concept page documents the Lake Ontario (CFB Trenton, Quinte West) - Harbour Chart (1:9,000) nautical chart and its integration with GPSNauticalCharts' multi-platform apps. The 1:9,000 scale chart covers coordinates from latitude 44.07 to 44.12 and longitude -77.58 to -77.48, providing detailed hydrographic data for maritime navigation in the region. ^[Source: Title | https://www.gpsnauticalcharts.com/main/nautical-chart/cagps0usir17fy9pxc1yqjpp-lake-ontario-cfb-trenton-quinte-west-harbour-chart-1-9-000-nautical-chart.html]

Key features include:
- Wind speed and direction data (measured in meters/second and true direction) ^[Source: Title | https://www.gpsnauticalcharts.com/main/nautical-chart/cagps0usir17fy9pxc1yqjpp-lake-ontario-cfb-trenton-quinte-west-harbour-chart-1-9-000-nautical-chart.html]
- Customizable depth shading and relief mapping
- Automatic slip-to-slip boat routing based on draft
- Integration with AIS receivers and NMEA protocols
