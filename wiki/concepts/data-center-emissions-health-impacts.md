### Health Impacts of Data Center Emissions

Data center emissions, particularly from backup diesel generators, have been linked to respiratory issues and premature deaths. According to a report by Community & Environmental Defense Services (CEDS), emissions from data centers can cause breathing problems for residents living within 0.6 miles of these facilities, with risks increasing near multiple data centers. Particulate matter and nitrogen dioxide emissions are cited as primary contributors to lung damage and mortality, with estimates suggesting 100,000-200,000 premature deaths annually from these particles alone.^[Source: Data centers air pollution associated with lung issues Report.pdf, Page 2]

### Regulatory Context

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) has highlighted the public health risks associated with data center emissions, particularly during periods of high demand when backup generators are frequently used. The Department of Energy (DOE) has issued emergency orders directing data centers to maximize backup generation output to prevent load interruptions, further emphasizing the health and safety concerns linked to these facilities.^[Source: Clean Air Act Resources for Data Centers US EPA.pdf, Page 1]
