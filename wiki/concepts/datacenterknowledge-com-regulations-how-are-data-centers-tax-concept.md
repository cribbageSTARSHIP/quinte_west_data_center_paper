Data centers face varying tax structures across jurisdictions, with key considerations including:

1. **Municipal tax abatements** - Many regions offer incentives to attract data center investments, though these often come with PILOT (Payment in Lieu of Taxes) agreements requiring annual payments to municipalities.
2. **Sales tax exemptions** - Facilities may qualify for exemptions on IT equipment, but this often excludes auxiliary infrastructure like cooling systems and electrical upgrades.
