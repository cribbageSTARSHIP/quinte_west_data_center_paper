Canada's Responsible Data Centre Development Principles establish a national framework for data centre projects, with five key expectations:

1. **Lasting Local Benefits**: Projects must create enduring economic and community advantages.
2. **No Electricity Cost Shifting**: Data centres must not pass on energy costs to ratepayers.
3. **Minimized Water Use**: Requirements to reduce environmental impact through water conservation.
4. **Transparency**: Projects must disclose local impacts and operations.
5. **Strategic Value**: Development must align with Canada's digital infrastructure goals.

These principles, supported by industry leaders like AWS, Meta, and Microsoft, aim to ensure responsible AI infrastructure growth while protecting community interests.
