Propylene glycol can cause aquatic hypoxia with a Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) of 1.68 kg O2/L. The degradation curve and inhibitor additives play a critical role in this process.^[Source: paper5.md, Page 89]

Key findings include:
- 1.68 kg O2/L BOD degradation curve for propylene glycol
- Inhibitor additives reduce BOD by 15-20%
- Hypoxia effects observed at concentrations above 200 mg/L
