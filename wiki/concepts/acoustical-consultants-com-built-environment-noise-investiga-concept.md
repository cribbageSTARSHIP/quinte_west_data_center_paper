A-weighting (dBA) and C-weighting (dBC) are frequency-weighted sound level measurements used in noise investigations:

- **A-weighting** emphasizes frequencies audible to the human ear (20Hz-20kHz), with greater sensitivity to mid-range frequencies (1kHz-4kHz). It aligns with perceived loudness and is commonly used for environmental noise assessments.
- **C-weighting** has a flatter frequency response (32Hz-12.5kHz), making it suitable for measuring low-frequency noise (e.g., from data center chillers or industrial equipment) that may be masked by A-weighting.
