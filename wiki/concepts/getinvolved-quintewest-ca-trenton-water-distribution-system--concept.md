# Trenton Water Distribution System Infrastructure

The Trenton Water Distribution System (WDS) serves approximately 27,800 people in the Trenton community and part of CFB Trenton, with connections to the Bayside and Carrying Place/Consecon systems in Prince Edward County. Key infrastructure components include:

- **Treatment Capacity**: 35,800 cubic meters/day through a conventional chemically assisted filtration plant [[concepts/quinteconservation-ca-who-we-are-resources-protecting-drinki-concept.md]]
- **Storage**: Two pressure zones with 2,273 cu.m elevated tanks (Oak Street and 2nd Dug Hill Road) [[entities/quinteconservation-ca-who-we-are-resources-protecting-drinki-entity.md]]
- **Pumps**: Four highlift pumps and five booster stations including Mount Pelion (replacement planned) and Trenton-Frankford [[entities/gov-mb-ca-nrnd-fish-wildlife-cdc-index-html-entity.md]]
- **Upgrades**: New Mount Pelion pump station and storage to align with the Water and Wastewater Master Plan [[entities/laballey-com-blog-propylene-glycol-safety-hazards-entity.md]]
