# Data Center Taxation Frameworks

This concept page synthesizes the Tax Foundation's research on state and local tax policies affecting data centers. Key findings include:

1. **Sales Tax Exemptions**: Most states exempt data center equipment from sales tax, but often require meeting economic development targets (e.g., job creation, investment thresholds). California uniquely applies sales tax to data centers.
2. **Tangible Personal Property (TPP) Taxes**: Over 20% of total tax burdens in 12 jurisdictions examined, with depreciation rules varying widely (e.g., Loudoun County, VA: 50% first-year tax; Santa Clara County, CA: 73% first-year tax).
3. **Corporate Tax Apportionment**: States using payroll/property factors (vs. sales-only) increase tax liability for data centers, creating double taxation risks.
4. **Real Property Taxes**: Land and structures incur significant costs, though some locales offer abatements.
