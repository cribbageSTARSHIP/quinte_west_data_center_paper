AI data center expansion is fundamentally constrained by electrical infrastructure capacity rather than semiconductor availability. The article highlights two key industrial beneficiaries: GE Vernova (GEV) and Eaton (ETN). GE Vernova's 100-gigawatt gas-turbine backlog through 2030 demonstrates the structural shift toward behind-the-meter power solutions, with pricing power shifting to manufacturers. Eaton's electrical sector backlog surged to $19.6 billion in 2026, driven by 200% spike in data center orders. Both companies face margin compression due to capital expenditures required to scale production, with GE Vernova targeting $600/kW turbine pricing by 2027 (200% increase from 2019) and Eaton acquiring Boyd Thermal to dominate high-margin liquid cooling.
