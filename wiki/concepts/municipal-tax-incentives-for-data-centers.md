### Municipal Tax Incentives for Data Centers

The US data center boom is fueled by state tax incentives and federal directives [[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 2]]. Total government revenues from data centers increased from $66B (2017) to $162B (2023), though many states offer sizable tax incentives to attract facilities [[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 11]]. Virginia's 2009 sales tax exemption for data center equipment exemplifies this trend [[Source: Health implications of the rapid rise of data centers in Virginia an exploratory assessment.pdf, Page 2]].
