# Municipal Tax Revenue from Hyperscale Data Centers in Alberta

The proposed $10B Synapse data center in Olds, Alberta could generate millions in property tax revenue for the Town of Olds, according to Councillor Brent Williams [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854). Key findings from the 2026 assessment services presentation:

1. **Revenue Projections**: Estimated $19.5M/year in tax revenue if 15% of the $10B project value is assessable [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854)
2. **Assessment Challenges**: Data centers are new inventory type with non-assessable computer equipment costing majority of project [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854)
3. **Provincial Levy**: 2% tax levy effective Dec 31, 2026 may apply to new assessments [[1]](https://www.thealbertan.com/olds-news/town-of-olds-could-reap-millions-of-dollars-in-property-taxes-from-data-centre-12301854)
