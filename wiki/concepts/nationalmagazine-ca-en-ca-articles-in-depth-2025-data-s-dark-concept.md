Artificial intelligence data centers consume vast quantities of fresh water for cooling, with estimates suggesting global AI demand could require 6.4 trillion liters annually by 2027. This usage raises concerns about sustainability and regulatory oversight, particularly in water-scarce regions like Alberta.
