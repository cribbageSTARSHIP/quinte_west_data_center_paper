### Water Consumption in AI Data Centers

From 2019 to 2023, total evaporated water used by California data centers increased by **96%**, reaching **49.9 billion liters annually** (equivalent to 20,000 Olympic-sized swimming pools). This includes both direct cooling evaporation and indirect evaporation from electricity generation^[Source: California data center health impacts tripled in 4 years.pdf, Page 4]^[Source: California data center health impacts tripled in 4 years.pdf, Page 5].

Key findings:
- Indirect evaporation (from electricity generation) accounts for most water use
- By 2028, projected water use could rise to **116 billion liters/year**
- This strains water resources in drought-prone regions
