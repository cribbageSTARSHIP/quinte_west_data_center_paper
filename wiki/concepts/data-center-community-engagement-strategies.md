### Data Center Community Engagement Strategies

Leading data center operators emphasize earning community trust through early engagement, local resource protection, and shared economic benefits [[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 13]]. OVHcloud's approach in Canada includes circular operations and long-term industrial investments [[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 13]]. Prince George's County (MD) established a data center task force for procedural protections [[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 12]].
