The electricity demand from data centers is projected to consume up to 12% of all U.S. electricity by 2028, with Virginia's data centers already accounting for 26% of the state's total electricity supply in 2023^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger their local communities and the country as a whole.pdf, Page 1]. This unprecedented consumption creates significant strain on the power grid, with the U.S. Department of Energy forecasting increased pressure on energy infrastructure as data center growth accelerates^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger their local communities and the country as a whole.pdf, Page 1].
