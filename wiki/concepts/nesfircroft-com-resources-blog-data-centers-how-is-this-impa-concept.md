# Data Center Energy Impact on Canada's Grid

## Key Statistics
- Canada's electricity grid derives >80% from low-emission sources [[1]](https://www.nesfircroft.com/resources/blog/data-centers-how-is-this-impacting-the-energy-mix-for-canada/)
- Hydroelectric power provides ~60% of national supply [[1]](https://www.nesfircroft.com/resources/blog/data-centers-how-is-this-impacting-the-energy-mix-for-canada/)
- IESO forecasts 400% increase in data center energy use by 2050 [[1]](https://www.nesfircroft.com/resources/blog/data-centers-how-is-this-impacting-the-energy-mix-for-canada/)

## Grid Challenges
- Ontario, Quebec, and BC face distinct capacity challenges [[1]](https://www.nesfircroft.com/resources/blog/data-centers-how-is-this-impacting-the-energy-mix-for-canada/)
- Alberta's proposed Bill 8 enables data center independent generation [[1]](https://www.nesfircroft.com/resources/blog/data-centers-how-is-this-impacting-the-energy-mix-for-canada/)
