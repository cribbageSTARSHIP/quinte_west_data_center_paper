# Virginia Data Center Energy Demand and Environmental Impact

## Energy Consumption

Virginia is the largest importer of electricity in the U.S., with data centers driving significant energy demand. A 2025 report by the Natural Resources Defense Council highlights the need for heat safety standards amid federal proposal uncertainties ^[Source: 08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 1269].

## Environmental Impact

The environmental footprint of data centers in the U.S. includes significant water usage and pollution. A 2023 study by Ortiz et al. found public health benefits from zero-emission electric power generation in Virginia, with estimated savings from reduced emissions ^[Source: 08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 1300].
