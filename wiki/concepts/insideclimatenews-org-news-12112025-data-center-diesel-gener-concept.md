This concept page examines the noise pollution generated by diesel generators at hyperscale data centers, focusing on their environmental and public health impacts. Key areas of concern include:

- **Low-frequency noise** (<100 Hz) from backup generators, which can penetrate buildings and cause sleep disturbance and cardiovascular stress^[Source: Title | https://insideclimatenews.org/news/12112025/data-center-diesel-generators-noise-pollution/].
- **Regulatory challenges** in quantifying noise levels using dBA vs. dBC measurements, as low-frequency noise is often underestimated by standard acoustic metrics^[Source: Title | https://insideclimatenews.org/news/12112025/data-center-diesel-generators-noise-pollution/].
- **Municipal ordinances** and industry standards for noise control from data center operations, including diesel generator emissions and thermal discharge.
