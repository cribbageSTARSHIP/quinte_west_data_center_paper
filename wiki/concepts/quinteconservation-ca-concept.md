This concept page synthesizes Quinte Conservation's environmental management strategies, focusing on water ecology interventions and infrastructure safety protocols.

Key initiatives include:

1. **Invasive Species Control**: Herbicide application for Water Soldier eradication in Bay of Quinte (^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/])
2. **Dam Safety Operations**: Annual dam maintenance in Moira/Napanee regions (^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/])
3. **Environmental Assessments**: Ongoing Class Environmental Assessment for Third Depot Lake Dam (^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/])

These activities align with [[entities/quinteconservation-ca-entity.md]]'s mandate to balance ecological preservation with infrastructure safety.
