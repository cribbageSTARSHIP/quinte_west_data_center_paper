Two proposed Alberta data centres (Wonder Valley and Synapse) were deemed exempt from provincial EA requirements under EPEA, with Directors citing factors like project scale and existing legislation. Federal IA exemptions apply if effects are addressed by existing laws. Section 44(3) of EPEA lists factors for EA exemptions, including location, public concerns, and similar projects in the area.
