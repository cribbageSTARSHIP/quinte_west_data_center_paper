This page documents economic analyses of data center health impacts:

- Vantage Data Center in Virginia: $53M-$99M annual health damages (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 4])
- 3.4-6.5 premature deaths/year in impacted regions (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 5])
- $14.1M projected public health cost reduction by 2028 under EPA guidelines (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 3])
- California data centers' health impacts tripled 2019-2023 (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 7])
- 72% potential increase in health impacts by 2028 without mitigation (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 7])
