Canada's Responsible Data Centre Development Principles mandate transparent reporting of water use and environmental impacts [[water-consumption-in-ai-data-centers]]. Proponents must provide independently verifiable information about local water use [[canadas-data-center-water-policies]].

Key requirements include:

- [[water-consumption-in-ai-data-centers]]: Water use must be measured and reported using recognized standards (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 3])
- [[02-water-ecology]]: Facilities must leverage waste heat as renewable energy (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 8])
- [[02-water-ecology]]: Data centers must minimize water use and environmental impact (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 9])
