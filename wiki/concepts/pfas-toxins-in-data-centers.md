# PFAS Toxins in Data Centers

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), known as "forever chemicals," are used in data center cooling systems and electronic components. Their persistence in the environment and potential to accumulate in the food chain pose significant risks:

- **Health impacts**: Linked to cancer, thyroid disease, and weakened immune systems [[Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks]].
- **Environmental contamination**: PFAS can leach into groundwater and surface water, affecting ecosystems and drinking water supplies [[08-hazards-emergency]].
- **Regulatory challenges**: Current policies often lack enforceable limits on PFAS emissions from data centers [[Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks]].
