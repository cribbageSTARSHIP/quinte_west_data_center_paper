# Water Ecology Impacts of Hyperscale AI Data Centers

Data centers consume vast quantities of water for cooling, with global projections estimating 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters annually by 2027. In northern Virginia, data center water use more than doubled between 2019-2023, consuming nearly 2 billion gallons in 2023 alone. Evaporative cooling processes produce wastewater with high contaminant concentrations, as seen in Meta's case of bacteria-contaminated water discharge. In Alberta, powering proposed data centers could consume 833,000-5.6 million liters annually per MW, with 20,000+ MW proposals equivalent to 234,000-1.6 million Albertans' annual water use. CCS technologies could increase water consumption by 29-81% at power plants.
