# Greenhouse Gas Emissions Regulation

Alberta's TIER system requires large emitters (≥100,000 CO2e/year) to reduce GHG intensity. Data center co-located power plants may trigger:

- EPEA Schedule of Activities (water diversions, power generation)
- Clean Electricity Regulations for fossil fuel emissions
- Section 109 prohibitions on adverse environmental releases
