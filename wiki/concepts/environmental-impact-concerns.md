# Environmental Impact Concerns

Public opposition to data centers centers on environmental risks including:

1. **Water consumption**: Hyperscale facilities require 833,000-5.6 million liters annually per MW for cooling and power generation [[water-consumption-from-power-generation]]^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 7].
2. **Climate impacts**: Increased natural gas extraction and carbon emissions from power generation raise health and climate risks [[health-climate-costs]]^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 7].
3. **Noise and land use**: Data centers contribute noise pollution (low-frequency hum <100 Hz) and alter natural/agricultural landscapes [[acoustics-dba-dbc, land-alteration]]^[Source: AI Data Centers Broad Optimism Meets Local Hesitation.pdf, Page 1].

Surveys show 81% of Canadians fear higher electricity bills from data centers, while 79% worry about environmental impacts [[affordability-sustainability]]^[Source: AI Data Centers Broad Optimism Meets Local Hesitation.pdf, Page 2].
