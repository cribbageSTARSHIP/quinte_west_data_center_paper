### Temporary Construction Jobs vs Permanent Staffing

Data center construction generates hundreds of temporary jobs for technicians, electricians, and trade workers, but permanent staffing remains limited. According to a review of 1,200 U.S. data centers, even the largest facilities employ fewer than 150 permanent workers.^[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 9]

#### Key Findings:
- Construction jobs are typically temporary and project-based.
- Permanent roles are limited to technicians, security, and facility management.
- Salaries for permanent roles range from $74,000 (technician) to $160,000 (operations manager) at some facilities.
- Subsidy-per-job ratios vary by region and regulatory framework.
