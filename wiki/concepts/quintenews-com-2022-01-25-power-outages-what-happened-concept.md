# Transformer Station Failure and Grid Resilience

^[Source: https://www.quintenews.com/2022/01/25/power-outages-what-happened/]

A cascading failure originating at a Hydro One transformer station triggered a grid instability event affecting 18,000 customers across Quinte West. The incident highlights:

1. **Grid Interdependency** - The failure at the Hydro One station [[entities/hydro-one.md]] propagated to Elexicon Energy's Harder Drive substation [[entities/elexicon-energy.md]], demonstrating the vulnerability of interconnected power networks.

2. **Restoration Strategy** - Elexicon's methodical approach to power restoration (section-by-section resupply) aligns with IESO's grid recovery protocols [[entities/ieso-ca-en-learn-ontario-power-system-overview-of-the-grid-entity.md]] to prevent system overload.

3. **Capacity Constraints** - The incident occurred during peak demand hours (1:30 PM), underscoring the need for enhanced substation capacity as outlined in Ontario Statute 90A31 [[concepts/ontario-ca-laws-statute-90a31-concept.md]].
