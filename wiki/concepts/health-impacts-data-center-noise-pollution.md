Prolonged exposure to data center noise (40-59 dB) correlates with cardiovascular risks, sleep disruption, and cognitive deficits in children [^[Source: raw/papers/04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md, Page 1480]]. Studies show noise above 55 dBA (day-night average) impairs children's reading comprehension and concentration [^[Source: raw/papers/04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md, Page 1493]]. Chronic exposure also increases hypertension risk and endocrine disruption [^[Source: raw/papers/04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md, Page 1481]].
