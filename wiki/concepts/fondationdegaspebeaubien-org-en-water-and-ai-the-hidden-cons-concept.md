# Water Usage in AI Data Centers

Canada's AI infrastructure depends on massive water consumption for cooling data centers. The International Energy Agency estimates global data center electricity demand will rise 15% annually through 2030, with water usage reaching **99.34 billion liters/year** by 2031 ([Source: https://www.fondationdegaspebeaubien.org/en/water-and-ai-the-hidden-constraint-behind-canadas-digital-future/]).

## Evaporative Cooling Trade-offs
80-90% of data center water use is lost to evaporation during cooling, creating pressure on local water supplies. This contrasts with innovative approaches like Japan's **White Data Center**, which uses stored snowmelt for cooling and redirects waste heat to aquaculture ([Source: https://www.fondationdegaspebeaubien.org/en/water-and-ai-the-hidden-constraint-behind-canadas-digital-future/]).

## Equity Concerns
Water competition disproportionately impacts rural/Indigenous communities. The article highlights the need for **targeted philanthropy** to ensure equitable access to water resources ([Source: https://www.fondationdegaspebeaubien.org/en/water-and-ai-the-hidden-constraint-behind-canadas-digital-future/]).
