# Ontario Pollution Reporting Process

Ontario's pollution reporting framework requires four key steps:

1. **Immediate Reporting** via phone (1-800-466-2266) or online tool for:
   - Illegal waste dumping
   - Pesticide misuse
   - Air/water/land pollution incidents

2. **Regulatory Requirements** for spill owners (per Ontario Regulation 675/98):
   - Report if you caused/permitted the spill
   - Have control of the substance pre-spill
   - Are a public agency with unreported spills

3. **Mandatory Information** for all reports:
   - Name/phone of reporter and spill controller
   - Date/time/location/duration of spill
   - Pollutant type/quantity/hazard level
   - Environmental conditions/weather
   - Actions taken to remediate

4. **Follow-up Process**:
   - Environmental officer assessment
   - Required cleanup by responsible parties
   - Province may recover cleanup costs
