# BC Data Center Tax Revenue and Fiscal Sustainability

The article discusses how British Columbia's local governments are advocating for higher property taxes on AI data centers to ensure communities benefit from their development. Current property tax classifications treat data centers as regular businesses (same as stores and clinics), but municipalities like Mackenzie argue they should be classified as light industry to generate more tax revenue.

^[Source: https://canadian-accountant.com/content/business/bc-taxes-ai-data-centres]

Key points:
- Data centers consume ~100MW of power (equivalent to 75,000 homes)
- Current tax rates generate minimal revenue for municipalities
- Proposed reclassification as light industry would triple revenue in some areas
- Five hyperscale data centers planned in BC (Telus, Bell Canada, Iren)
- UBCM supports tax reform to address infrastructure costs and environmental impacts
