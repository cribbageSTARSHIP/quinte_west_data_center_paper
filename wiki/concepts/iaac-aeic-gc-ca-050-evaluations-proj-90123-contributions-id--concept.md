# Environmental and Social Impacts of Hyperscale Data Centers

The Beacon AI Centers Heartland Project comment highlights critical issues with data center development:

1. **Resource Consumption**: Data centers consume 1500 cubic meters of water daily, straining local resources during droughts [[wikilinks]][concepts/canada-ca-en-health-canada-services-publications-healthy-liv-concept.md]. This aligns with [[wikilinks]][concepts/responsible-data-centre-development.md] concerns about water usage.

2. **Community Impact**: Short-term construction jobs (200-300 positions) are offset by long-term operational job losses, with no lasting local economic benefit [[wikilinks]][entities/climatefast-ca-project-2025-12-say-no-ai-data-centres-entity.md].

3. **Environmental Harm**: Habitat disruption, pollution, and noise/light impacts on wildlife and residents are documented [[wikilinks]][concepts/air-pollutant-emissions-in-canada.md].
