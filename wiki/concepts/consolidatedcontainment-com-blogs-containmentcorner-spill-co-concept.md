# Data Center Fuel System Spill Containment

Consolidated Containment specializes in spill containment solutions for data center fuel systems, addressing the unique challenges posed by backup diesel generators and liquid cooling systems. Our solutions incorporate:

1. **Primary Containment Barriers** - Custom-engineered secondary containment basins with 100% overflow capacity for diesel fuel storage tanks (per API 2015 standards)
2. **Spill Response Kits** - Automated spill detection systems integrated with EPA-compliant containment berms
3. **Emergency Mitigation** - AI-powered predictive analytics for leak detection in fuel lines and cooling systems
