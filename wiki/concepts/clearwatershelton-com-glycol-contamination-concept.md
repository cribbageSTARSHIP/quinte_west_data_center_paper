# Glycol Contamination: Industrial Risks and Mitigation

Glycol contamination in industrial systems poses significant risks to infrastructure, safety, and environmental health. This concept explores:

1. **Contamination pathways**: Cross-contamination with potable water, system leaks, improper maintenance, material incompatibility, and external spills (^[Source: https://clearwatershelton.com/glycol-contamination/])
2. **Health impacts**: Ethylene glycol toxicity (acute renal failure risk), propylene glycol exposure effects (^[Source: https://clearwatershelton.com/glycol-contamination/])
3. **Prevention strategies**: Material compatibility checks, routine monitoring (refractometry, gas chromatography), and system design isolation (^[Source: https://clearwatershelton.com/glycol-contamination/])
