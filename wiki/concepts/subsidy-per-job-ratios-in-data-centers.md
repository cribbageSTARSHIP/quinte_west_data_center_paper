### Subsidy-Per-Job Ratios

Data center development often involves tax abatements, PILOT agreements, and sales tax exemptions. Subsidy-per-job ratios vary significantly by region and facility size. For example:

- In Lancaster, PA, a community benefits agreement required $10 million for economic development and climate goals per data center.^[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 12]
- Temporary construction jobs typically receive fewer subsidies compared to permanent staffing.
- Subsidies may be tied to clean energy commitments, noise mitigation, or local employment programs.
