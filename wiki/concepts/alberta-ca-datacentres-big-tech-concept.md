# Alberta's Data Centre Regulatory Framework

Alberta employs a competitive, non-subsidized approach to attract data centre investment while maintaining local control over resources. Key features include:

1. **No public subsidies**: No tax holidays, discounted power, or direct financial incentives are offered to data centre developers [[1]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

2. **Data centre levy**: A 2% levy on computing equipment value is applied, fully deductible against Alberta corporate income taxes [[2]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

3. **Resource ownership**: Water rights remain with the Crown, farmland acquisition is restricted by foreign ownership regulations, and grid access is regulated through independent oversight [[3]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

4. **Multi-tier approval process**: Projects require approvals from local councils, utilities commissions, environmental regulators, and grid operators - any single entity can block a project [[4]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).

5. **Value capture**: Alberta retains economic benefits through royalties, property taxes, and infrastructure development while requiring private sector investment in power plants, pipelines, and fibre routes [[5]](https://www.alberta.ca/datacentres/big-tech).
