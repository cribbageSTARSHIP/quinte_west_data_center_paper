**Key Tax Revenue Dynamics:**
- Current property tax classification: Data centers classified as businesses (same rate as retail, dental clinics, law offices)
- Proposed reclassification: Light industry (would triple Mackenzie's revenue from Iren facility)
- Fiscal implications: Hyperscale data centers consume 100+ MW (equivalent to 75,000 homes) but pay business rates
- UBCM endorsement: Union of BC Municipalities supports tax reform for equitable revenue distribution

**Impact Analysis:**
- Current model: $250,000/year in community benefits (non-binding)
- Revenue potential: Light industry classification could generate $2.5M/year for Mackenzie
- Zoning challenges: 75% of BC municipalities lack specific data center regulations
