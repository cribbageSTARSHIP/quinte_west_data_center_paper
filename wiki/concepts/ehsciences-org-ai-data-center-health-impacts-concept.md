# AI Data Center Health Impacts

This concept page analyzes the health implications of hyperscale AI data centers as documented in [Source: AI Data Center Health Impacts | https://ehsciences.org/ai-data-center-health-impacts/]. The analysis covers:

1. **Cardiovascular stress** from low-frequency noise (below 100 Hz) and thermal discharge impacts^[Source: AI Data Center Health Impacts | https://ehsciences.org/ai-data-center-health-impacts/]
2. **Respiratory risks** from particulate plumes and PFAS exposure during backup generator operations^[Source: AI Data Center Health Impacts | https://ehsciences.org/ai-data-center-health-impacts/]
3. **Neurological effects** of sleep disruption from continuous acoustic pressure levels exceeding 55 dBA^[Source: AI Data Center Health Impacts | https://ehsciences.org/ai-data-center-health-impacts/]
