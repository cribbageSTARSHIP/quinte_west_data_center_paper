# Environmental and Regulatory Challenges of AI Data Centers in Alberta

This concept page synthesizes key findings from the Environmental Law Centre's analysis of Alberta's data center regulatory landscape. The article highlights systemic gaps in environmental assessment requirements, local opposition to projects like the Wonder Valley AI data center, and the urgent need for statutory reforms to address water usage, power grid strain, and hazardous waste management.

^[Source: https://elc.ab.ca/post-library/are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-centres/]

## Key Regulatory Gaps
1. **Environmental Assessment Exemptions**: Data centers are currently exempt from mandatory provincial environmental assessments despite significant impacts on water resources and land use (see [[entities/albertawilderness-ca-issues-wildlands-energy-hyperscale-ai-d-entity.md]]).
2. **Water Usage**: The Wonder Valley project was granted a preliminary water licence for 24M m³/year, raising concerns about wetland disturbance and compliance with the Wetland Policy (^[Source: https://elc.ab.ca/post-library/are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-centres/]).
3. **Power Grid Strain**: The AESO's 1,200 MW allocation cap creates a bottleneck for new data center projects, with developers prioritizing grid-connected models over self-generated power solutions (see [[entities/iaac-aeic-gc-ca-050-evaluations-proj-90123-contributions-id--entity.md]].

## Local Opposition
Community resistance has blocked or delayed multiple projects, including:
- The Synapse Data Center in Olds (^[Source: https://elc.ab.ca/post-library/are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-centres/])
- The Kineticor proposal in Rocky View County (^[Source: https://elc.ab.ca/post-library/are-we-being-smart-about-ai-data-centres/])

## Recommendations
The ELC advocates for:
1. Mandatory environmental assessments under the Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act
2. Independent water licensing for data centers
3. Stricter emissions controls under the Technology Innovation and Emissions Reduction Regulation
