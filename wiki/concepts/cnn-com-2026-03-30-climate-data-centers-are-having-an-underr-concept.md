Data centers powering artificial intelligence create "heat islands," warming surrounding land by up to 16°F and affecting over 340 million people [[wikilinks]](entities/cnn-com-2026-03-30-climate-data-centers-are-having-an-underr-entity.md). Research shows surface temperatures increase by 3.6°F on average after data center operations begin, with extreme cases reaching 16.4°F [[wikilinks]](concepts/responsible-data-centre-development.md). These effects extend up to 6.2 miles from facilities [[Source: Title | https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/30/climate/data-centers-are-having-an-underrported]].
