**Respiratory Health Risks from Data Center Emissions**

Gas-powered data centers release nitrogen oxides (NOx) that exacerbate respiratory conditions like asthma and COPD. Fine particulate matter (PM2.5), similar to wildfire smoke, enters the bloodstream and contributes to heart attacks and strokes. Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) like formaldehyde and benzene increase cancer risks. These pollutants disproportionately affect rural and Indigenous communities.
