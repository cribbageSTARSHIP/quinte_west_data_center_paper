# Regulatory Challenges in Hyperscale Data Center Development

Hyperscale data centers face complex regulatory challenges, including compliance with noise control rules (Rule 012) and water usage regulations. Power plant approvals require adherence to Rule 007: Facility Applications, which may involve municipal permits and zoning adjustments. ^[Source: Alberta AI Data Centres Environmental and Regulatory Concerns.pdf, Page 7]

Regulatory frameworks must address both noise and water consumption issues, as highlighted by the call for mandatory environmental impact assessments (EIAs) to ensure sustainable development. ^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 10]
