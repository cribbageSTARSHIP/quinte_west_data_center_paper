Ecological Integrity refers to how "natural" an area is, encompassing three components:
1. **Structure**: The physical and biological components of an ecosystem (rocks, water, plants, wildlife).
2. **Composition**: Biodiversity, measured by the number of native species.
3. **Function**: Natural processes like predator-prey dynamics and water cycles that sustain biodiversity.

Ontario Parks prioritizes these components through:
- Designated trails to protect sensitive dune areas (^[Source: Title | https://www.quintenews.com/2026/06/09/maintaining-ecological-integrity-at-ontario-parks/])
- Volunteer-led invasive species removal (^[Source: Title | https://www.quintenews.com/2026/06/09/maintaining-ecological-integrity-at-ontario-parks/])
- Native plant restoration programs
