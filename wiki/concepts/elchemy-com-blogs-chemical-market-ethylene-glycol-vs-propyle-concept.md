# Ethylene Glycol vs Propylene Glycol: Key Industrial Cooling Tradeoffs

## Thermal Performance
Ethylene glycol delivers **20-25% higher thermal conductivity** (0.252 W/m·K vs 0.200 W/m·K at 20°C) and **lower viscosity** (27 cP vs 96 cP at -10°F), enabling **superior heat transfer efficiency** in closed-loop systems^[Source: https://elchemy.com/blogs/chemical-market/ethylene-glycol-vs-propylene-glycol-which-is-more-sustainable-for-industrial-cooling]. This makes it preferred for data centers, manufacturing chillers, and automotive antifreeze applications.

## Environmental Impact
Propylene glycol offers **61% lower greenhouse gas emissions** when bio-based and **faster biodegradation** (1-7 days vs 10-30 days for ethylene glycol). It is **FDA GRAS-certified** for food contact and avoids SARA/CERCLA spill reporting requirements^[Source: https://elchemy.com/blogs/chemical-market/ethylene-glycol-vs-propylene-glycol-which-is-more-sustainable-for-industrial-cooling].

## Application Requirements
- **Closed-loop systems**: Ethylene glycol dominates due to efficiency (e.g., automotive engines, HVAC systems)
- **Open-loop/food-grade**: Propylene glycol is mandatory (e.g., pharmaceutical cooling, aircraft deicing)
- **Bio-based propylene glycol**: Achieves **61% GHG reduction** vs petroleum-based variants^[Source: https://elchemy.com/blogs/chemical-market/ethylene-glycol-vs-propylene-glycol-which-is-more-sustainable-for-industrial-cooling]
