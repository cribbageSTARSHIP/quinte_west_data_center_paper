Data centers emit electromagnetic fields (EMF) from power infrastructure that disrupt honeybee navigation systems, reducing pollination efficiency by altering gene expression in navigation and foraging systems [[entities/environmentalhealthproject-org-post-the-dangers-of-data-cent-entity.md]]. Concurrently, heat emissions from data centers correlated with 3.5-14 million annual bee deaths in Australia via thermoregulatory stress and Varroa mite proliferation [[concepts/05-human-health.md]].
