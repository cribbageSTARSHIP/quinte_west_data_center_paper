# Water and Power Consumption in AI Data Centers

AI data centers consume vast amounts of water for evaporative cooling (up to 5 million gallons/day for large facilities) and electricity (equivalent to 100,000 homes). Cooling systems use 49 billion gallons/year in Texas alone, with 31% of Google's water use coming from high-scarcity watersheds. Power demands strain grids, requiring 50x more water for natural gas vs solar generation.
