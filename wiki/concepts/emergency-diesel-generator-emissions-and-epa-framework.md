### Regulatory Framework
The EPA's Tier 4 standards for diesel generators require particulate matter (PM) emissions below 0.01 g/bhp-hr [[Source: raw/papers/08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 606]].

### Emergency Use
Data centers maintain backup diesel generators for power outages, with routine testing contributing 35,000-51,000 annual hours of operation [[Source: raw/papers/08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 799]].

### Health Risks
NO2 and PM emissions from generators are linked to respiratory diseases and cardiovascular mortality [[Source: raw/papers/08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 814]].
