This concept page synthesizes findings from the 2021 Canadian report on air pollution health impacts. The report associates ambient air pollution with respiratory symptoms, disease development, and premature mortality across Canada.

Key pollutants analyzed include:
- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5)
- Ground-level ozone
- Nitrogen dioxide (NO2)

Scientific evidence links these pollutants to:
1. Respiratory symptoms (^[Source: Title | https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/publications/healthy-living/health-impacts-air-pollution-2021.html])
2. Chronic disease development
3. Premature mortality (estimated as a leading risk factor for disability)
