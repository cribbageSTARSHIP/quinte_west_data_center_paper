DataOne's unpermitted generator operations in New Jersey released approximately 140 tons of NOx annually, ranking it among the state's top NOx polluters [[concepts/ecology-wa-gov-air-climate-air-quality-data-centers-concept.md]]. NOx emissions are linked to asthma, heart attacks, and premature death ^[Source: Title | https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/01/us-datacenter-new-jersey-pollution].

Community residents reported health concerns and noise pollution equivalent to airplane sounds ^[Source: Title | https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/01/us-datacenter-new-jersey-pollution]. At full capacity, the generator fleet could emit over 1,500 tons of NOx/year, exceeding New Jersey's major source thresholds ^[Source: Title | https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/01/us-datacenter-new-jersey-pollution].

This case highlights regulatory gaps in permitting for facilities capable of emitting >25 tons NOx/year, which requires environmental reviews and public input ^[Source: Title | https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/oct/01/us-datacenter-new-jersey-pollution].
