Low-frequency noise (<100 Hz) generated by hyperscale cooling chiller arrays and electrical substation transformers exhibits high building-penetration characteristics and structural resonance^[Source: 04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md]. 

Standard municipal noise bylaws enforce A-weighted decibel (dBA) thresholds, which systematically discount frequencies below 1 kHz and fail to capture structural hum, vibroacoustic coupling, and sleep disruption^[Source: 04_acoustics_dba_dbc.md]. Environmental health studies document that chronic exposure to low-frequency C-weighted (dBC) peaks correlates with elevated nighttime cortisol, cardiovascular strain, and sleep disturbance in adjacent residential communities^[Source: 05_human_health.md].
