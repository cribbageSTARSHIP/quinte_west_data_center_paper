# Data Centre Environmental Impact Assessment

This concept page synthesizes key findings about the environmental and infrastructural implications of hyperscale AI data centers, with a focus on water usage and emergency risk management.

## Key Findings

1. **Water Consumption**: Data centers use evaporative cooling systems that can consume megalliters of municipal water daily, leading to aquifer depletion and thermal discharge into local waterways. See [[concepts/quinteconservation-ca-watershed-management-dams-concept.md]].

2. **Emergency Hazards**: Lithium-ion battery thermal runaway risks and toxic gas plumes require specialized municipal fire department readiness. See [[entities/association-of-municipalities-of-ontario-entity.md]].

3. **Provenance**: All claims sourced from [[raw/articles/policyalternatives-ca-news-research-so-youre-getting-a-data-.md]]
