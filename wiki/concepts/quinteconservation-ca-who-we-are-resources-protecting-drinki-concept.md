### Groundwater Contamination Risks
Homeowners with private wells bear responsibility for testing water safety due to shared groundwater resources. Contamination from one well can endanger others through aquifer depletion or chemical seepage (^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/who-we-are/resources/protecting-drinking-water/]).

### Private Well Management
During droughts, homeowners should:
1. Use water conservation strategies from Quinte Conservation's Low Water Resource Guide
2. Consider cisterns or water hauling services to reduce well strain
3. Follow boil water advisories strictly (^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/who-we-are/resources/protecting-drinking-water/])

### Testing Requirements
Annual testing for nitrates, bacteria, and contaminants is recommended. Private labs offer paid testing services for suspected chemical contamination (^[Source: Title | https://www.quinteconservation.ca/who-we-are/resources/protecting-drinking-water/]).
