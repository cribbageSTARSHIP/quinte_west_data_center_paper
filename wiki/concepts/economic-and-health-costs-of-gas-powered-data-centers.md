### Economic and Health Costs of Gas-Powered Data Centers

Gas-powered data centers impose **$53M–$99M/year in health damages** in densely populated areas (e.g., Northern Virginia) due to PM2.5 and VOCs (^[Source: Analyzing air pollution health economic risks from AI data centers Harvard TH Chan School of Public.pdf, Page 5]). Vulnerable populations—children, the elderly, and low-income communities—face **disproportionate risks** from pollution (^[Source: Analyzing air pollution health economic risks from AI data centers Harvard TH Chan School of Public.pdf, Page 4]).

The **xAI Gas Plant in Mississippi** is projected to add **tens of millions in annual health damages** (^[Source: Analyzing air pollution health economic risks from AI data centers Harvard TH Chan School of Public.pdf, Page 5]). These costs include **premature deaths, hospital admissions, and lost productivity**—burdens often unaccounted for in infrastructure planning (^[Source: Analyzing air pollution health economic risks from AI data centers Harvard TH Chan School of Public.pdf, Page 3]).
