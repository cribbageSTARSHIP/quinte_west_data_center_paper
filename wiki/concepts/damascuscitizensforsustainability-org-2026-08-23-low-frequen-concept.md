^[Source: Low-Frequency Noise from Data Centers Can Harm Your Health | https://www.damascuscitizensforsustainability.org/2026/08/23/low-frequency-noise-from-data-centers-can-harm-your-health/]

**Key Findings:**
1. Chronic exposure to low-frequency noise (20–500 Hz) from data center cooling systems and diesel generators correlates with cardiovascular risks, hypertension, and sleep disturbance.
2. dB(C) measurements are critical for capturing low-frequency noise impacts, as dB(A) scales underrepresent these frequencies.
3. Data center noise pollution exceeds WHO guidelines (~50 dBA) and can reach 80+ dBA, causing stress-related health effects.
4. Infrasound (0–20 Hz) and low-frequency noise are linked to vibroacoustic disease (VAD) with prolonged exposure.

**Measurement Standards:**
- Data center noise ordinances must specify both dBA and dBC thresholds at property lines.
- Cooling systems and backup generators contribute 10–20 dBA additional noise beyond A-weighted measurements.
