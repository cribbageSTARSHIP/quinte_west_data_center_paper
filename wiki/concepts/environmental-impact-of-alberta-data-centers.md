Alberta's data center industry presents significant environmental risks:

**Air Quality Concerns**
- Gas-powered data centers release harmful pollutants (NOx, PM2.5) from backup generators (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
- Alberta's 2025 CAPE report highlights health risks from particulate plumes (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 4])

**Water Usage Issues**
- Evaporative cooling systems consume 1-3 MGD per 100MW facility (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 1])
- No requirements for waste heat recovery or district energy systems (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 8])

**Regulatory Gaps**
- No mandatory environmental assessments for data centers under EPEA (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
- No transparency in water usage with current licensing model (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 1])
