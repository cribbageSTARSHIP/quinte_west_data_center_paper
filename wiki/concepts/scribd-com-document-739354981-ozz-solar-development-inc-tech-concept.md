OZZ Solar Development Inc. is proposing 5 solar PV carport projects totaling **1.276 MW DC** and 11 solar PV rooftop projects totaling **801.6 kW DC** for the City of Toronto. This aligns with the **01-power-grid** pillar, focusing on distributed energy generation capacity.
