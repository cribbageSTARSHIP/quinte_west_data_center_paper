A 2023 study by Molina-Montenegro et al. found that exposure to EMF radiation from high-voltage power lines induced physiological stress in honeybees, reducing pollination behavior and plant diversity (^[Source: Title | https://www.ehn.org/honeybee-emf-power-line-pollination]).

Key findings:
- EMF-exposed bees showed altered gene expression in navigation/foraging (^[Source: Title | https://www.ehn.org/honeybee-emf-power-line-pollination]).
- Reduced flower visits and seed production were observed only when towers were actively emitting EMF (^[Source: Title | https://www.ehn.org/honeybee-emf-power-line-pollination]).
- Impacts align with the global pollination crisis, risking crop production (^[Source: Title | https://www.ehn.org/honeybee-emf-power-line-pollination]).
