# Ontario Power Grid Management by IESO

The Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO) oversees Ontario's power grid, ensuring reliable electricity delivery through:

1. **Grid Operations**: Balancing supply and demand via real-time market data tracking [[concepts/01-power-grid.md]]
2. **Supply Mix Management**: Coordinating generation from nuclear, hydro, gas, and renewable sources [[concepts/supply-mix-generation.md]]
3. **Demand Response Programs**: Engaging consumers to adjust usage during peak periods [[concepts/demand-response.md]]
