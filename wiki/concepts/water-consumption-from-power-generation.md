Hyperscale data centres require **833,000 to 5.6 million litres of water annually per MW** for power generation and cooling, with CCS technologies increasing consumption by 29-81%^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 7]. At scale, 20,000 MW of data centre load would consume water equivalent to **234,000 to 1.6 million Albertans' annual use**^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 7].
