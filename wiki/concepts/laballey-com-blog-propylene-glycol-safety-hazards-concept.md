# Propylene Glycol Safety & Hazards

Propylene glycol (1,2-propanediol) is a versatile industrial chemical with a remarkably low toxicity profile. This concept page synthesizes key safety information from [[raw/articles/laballey-com-blog-propylene-glycol-safety-hazards.md]]:

1. **Toxicity Profile**: Oral LD50 of 20,000-22,000 mg/kg in rats, classified as GRAS by FDA for food/pharmaceutical use. Minimal health hazards under normal conditions, with primary concerns being mild eye/skin irritation and CNS depression at very high doses.^[Source: Title | https://www.laballey.com/blog/propylene-glycol-safety-hazards]

2. **Flammability**: Flash point of 103-107°C (217-224°F), non-flammable at room temperature. NFPA ratings: Health 0-1, Flammability 1, Reactivity 0.^[Source: Title | https://www.laballey.com/blog/propylene-glycol-safety-hazards]

3. **Handling Precautions**: Requires standard industrial hygiene practices - ventilate during heating, use PPE, avoid prolonged skin contact. Hygroscopic nature necessitates moisture protection.^[Source: Title | https://www.laballey.com/blog/propylene-glycol-safety-hazards]

4. **Environmental Impact**: Readily biodegradable, low toxicity to aquatic life, not classified as hazardous under EPA regulations. Miscible with water, may leach into groundwater but biodegrades rather than persists.^[Source: Title | https://www.laballey.com/blog/propylene-glycol-safety-hazards]

5. **Regulatory Status**: No OSHA PEL, not classified as hazardous by DOT for transportation. FDA GRAS status for food/pharmaceutical use when meeting USP/NF purity standards.^[Source: Title | https://www.laballey.com/blog/propylene-glycol-safety-hazards]
