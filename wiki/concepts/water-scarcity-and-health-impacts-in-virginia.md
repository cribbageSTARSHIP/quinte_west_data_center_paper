# Water Scarcity and Health Impacts in Virginia

Data centers in Virginia have significantly increased water demand, competing with existing needs for agriculture, public supply, and industrial operations. This has led to intensified stress on water resources, particularly in drought-prone regions. In 2023, data centers in Northern Virginia consumed nearly 2 billion gallons of water, a 63% increase since 2019. This exacerbates existing water scarcity issues, as Virginia has experienced prolonged drought conditions, reducing water availability and flow. Contaminants from cooling systems, such as sodium, magnesium, sulfate, and potassium, pose serious health risks, including skin irritation, diarrhea, and adverse fetal outcomes. Additionally, drought conditions promote pathogen growth, increasing risks of waterborne diseases like diarrheal illnesses and E. coli.
