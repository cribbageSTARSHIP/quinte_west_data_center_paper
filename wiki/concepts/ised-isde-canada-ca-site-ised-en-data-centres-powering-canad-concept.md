# Canada's Responsible Data Centre Development Principles

Canada's data centre industry operates under five core principles established by the Government of Canada to balance digital infrastructure growth with community, environmental, and economic responsibilities:

1. **Local Benefits**: Data centres must generate lasting community value through jobs, skills training, Indigenous partnerships, and local procurement [[1]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles). Proponents must engage communities early to align investments with local priorities.

2. **Cost Responsibility**: Projects must pay for grid upgrades and infrastructure directly attributable to their development, avoiding cost-shifting to households [[2]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

3. **Water Stewardship**: Facilities must minimize freshwater use and prioritize closed-loop cooling technologies [[3]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

4. **Transparency**: Proponents must disclose power/water usage, infrastructure needs, and track commitments using measurable, verifiable data [[4]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

5. **Strategic Value**: Projects must strengthen Canada's digital resilience, cybersecurity, and sovereign compute capacity while meeting legal protection requirements [[5]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles).

**Signatories** include AMD, AWS, Anthropic, and 40+ tech/energy firms [[6]](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/data-centres-powering-canadas-digital-future-responsibly/canadas-responsible-data-centre-development-principles). The framework aims to ensure AI infrastructure development aligns with Canadian values of community benefit, resource responsibility, and long-term competitiveness.
