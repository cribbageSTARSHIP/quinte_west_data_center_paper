The Rivers Inlet Diesel Spill occurred on **February 15, 2021**, when an estimated **7,000 L of diesel** was released into Rivers Inlet from a storage tank used to supply an emergency power generator^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].

### Response Efforts
- **February 16, 2021**: Two local Coastal Guardian Watch vessels were on site, and a Transport Canada National Area Surveillance Program (NASP) over-flight was ordered^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- **February 19, 2021**: A secondary containment boom was deployed, and environmental sensitivity assessments were conducted with First Nations representatives^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- **March 11, 2021**: Tanker trucks holding collected diesel were removed, and soil remediation plans continued with provincial and federal agencies^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].

### Key Entities Involved
- **B.C. Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Strategy (ENV)**: Oversaw spill response, coordinated with First Nations, and conducted environmental assessments^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- **Wuikinuxv First Nation and Heiltsuk First Nation**: Collaborated in spill response and sensitivity site identification^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- **Canadian Coast Guard (CCG)**: Assisted in spill containment and monitoring^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].

### Environmental Impact
- Diesel sheening was reported in Rivers Inlet, with containment booms deployed to prevent spread^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
- No wildlife distress was observed during initial assessments^[Source: Title | https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/spills-environmental-emergencies/spill-incidents/rivers-inlet-diesel-spill].
