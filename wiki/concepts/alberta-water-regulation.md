# Alberta Water Regulation for AI Data Centers

Alberta's regulatory framework for AI data centers includes mandatory environmental impact assessments (EIAs) and water usage regulations. The province has faced criticism for insufficient EIAs, leading to public concerns about water consumption for evaporative cooling and potential aquifer depletion [[water-ecology-impact]]^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 8].

Municipalities require water drawdown approvals under the Water Act, with specific focus on thermal discharge and aquifer depletion risks. The Alberta Energy Regulator (AER) oversees these processes, though critics argue current regulations do not adequately address water consumption for data center cooling [[02-water-ecology]]^[Source: Alberta AI Data Centres Environmental and Regulatory Concerns.pdf, Page 5].

Tax incentives for data centers, such as the 2% levy on computing equipment, are fully deductible against provincial corporate income taxes, reducing actual revenue contributions [[mihta-askiy-data-center]]^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 8].
