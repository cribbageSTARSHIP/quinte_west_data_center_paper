# Ontario Data Center Prioritization Regulation

The Ontario government proposes regulatory amendments to prioritize data center electricity connections that align with economic development goals. Key provisions include:

- **Economic Criteria**: Preference for projects creating high-quality jobs and supporting domestic data hosting^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001] 
- **Grid Management**: Regulation to manage connection requests and balance data center demand with other energy sectors^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001]
- **Environmental Consideration**: No material environmental impact expected from implementation^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001]
- **Local Benefits**: Evaluation of tax revenues, infrastructure investments, and employment opportunities^[Source: https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/025-1001]
