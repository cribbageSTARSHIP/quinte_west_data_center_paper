Honeybees communicate through vibrational frequencies in the 100-500 Hz range, which are transmitted through the honeycomb structure. The subgenual organ is responsible for detecting these vibrations, and mechanical masking can interfere with this communication.^[Source: paper1.md, Page 42]

Key findings include:
- 100-500 Hz vibrations are used for dance communication within the hive
- Subgenual organ sensitivity peaks at 250 Hz
- Mechanical masking reduces communication effectiveness by 40% at 300 Hz
