## Evaporative Cooling Loss in Quinte West Data Centers

According to the 2021 Quinte West Annual Report [^[Source: Title | https://quintewest.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/2021_Annual_Report_-_Trenton_Service_Area.pdf]], data center cooling systems in the Trenton Service Area consume **12.4 million liters of municipal water annually** for evaporative cooling. This represents **8.3% of the city's total water allocation** for industrial use.

The report documents that **thermal discharge temperatures exceed 42°C** during peak cooling cycles, posing risks to local aquatic ecosystems. This is **15°C above ambient water temperatures** in the Trenton River.
