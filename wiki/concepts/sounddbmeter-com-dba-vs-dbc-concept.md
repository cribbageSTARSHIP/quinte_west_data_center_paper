# Acoustic Measurement Weighting Standards (A-weighting vs C-weighting)

## Core Differences
- **dBA (A-weighting):** Emphasizes frequencies 500 Hz–6 kHz, aligns with human hearing sensitivity^[Source: https://sounddbmeter.com/dba-vs-dbc/].
- **dBC (C-weighting):** Captures full-frequency range (20 Hz–12.5 kHz), ideal for low-frequency noise analysis^[Source: https://sounddbmeter.com/dba-vs-dbc/].

## Applications
- **dBA:** Common for environmental noise assessments, traffic, and HVAC systems.
- **dBC:** Essential for industrial machinery, wind turbines, and subsonic noise evaluation.

## Regulatory Context
- ISO 1996 and ANSI S12.9 standards mandate C-weighting for low-frequency measurements in industrial zones.

## Technical Notes
- C-weighting reveals hidden low-frequency components often missed by A-weighting^[Source: https://sounddbmeter.com/dba-vs-dbc/].
- Modern sound level meters automatically switch between A/C weighting based on frequency range.
