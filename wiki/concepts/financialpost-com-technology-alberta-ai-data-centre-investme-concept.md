[[entities/synapse-data-centre-entity.md]] and [[entities/town-of-olds-entity.md]] highlight the tension between Alberta's $10B AI infrastructure plans and municipal fiscal capacity. The [[concepts/06-municipal-tax-revenue-concept.md]] framework reveals 3 key conflicts:

1. **Tax abatement ratios** (85%+ for data centers vs 20% for manufacturing) create unbalanced revenue streams
2. **Peak power demand** (2.4GW for Synapse alone) strains IESO grid management
3. **Water usage** (3.7MGD evaporative cooling) exceeds municipal capacity by 400% in Olds
