# Environmental Impact of Data Center EMFs on Honeybee Navigation

Honeybees rely on natural electromagnetic fields (EMFs) for navigation, but exposure to man-made EMFs from data center infrastructure may disrupt their behavior. Studies suggest that low-frequency EMFs from power lines and transformers could cause:

- Cellular stress in bees
- Reduced pollination efficiency
- Disrupted foraging patterns
