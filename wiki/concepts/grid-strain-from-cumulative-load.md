Data centre load requests in Alberta have surged to **20,000 MW**, exceeding the province's total generation capacity of 10,000 MW^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 3]. This creates a **9,900% increase in cumulative demand** since 2024, with supply capped at 1,200 MW by AESO to prevent grid instability^[Source: A moratorium on Ai data centres Canadians.pdf, Page 3].
