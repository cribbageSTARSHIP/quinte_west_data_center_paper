AI data centres powered by natural gas generators pose significant health and environmental risks:

1. **Air Pollution**: Release NOx linked to asthma and dementia, particulate matter causing cardiovascular issues, and VOCs increasing cancer risk^[Source: Title | https://cape.ca/resource/health-facts-gas-powered-data-centres/].
2. **Forever Chemicals**: PFAS in cooling systems linked to cancer and birth defects^[Source: Title | https://cape.ca/resource/health-facts-gas-powered-data-centres/].
3. **Water Use**: Millions of litres/day for cooling, straining water infrastructure in drought-prone areas^[Source: Title | https://cape.ca/resource/health-facts-gas-powered-data-centres/].
4. **Noise Pollution**: Continuous operations linked to hypertension and cognitive impairment^[Source: Title | https://cape.ca/resource/health-facts-gas-powered-data-centres/].
5. **Healthcare Strain**: Rural communities face increased health challenges with limited medical resources^[Source: Title | https://cape.ca/resource/health-facts-gas-powered-data-centres/].
