# Ontario Data Centre Playbook for Cities

The National Observer's [Ontario Data Centre Playbook](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/08/20/news/ontario-data-centre-playbook-cities-react) provides a framework for municipalities to engage with data center developers. Key provisions include:

1. **Municipal Engagement Requirements**
   Developers must demonstrate compliance with local land-use plans and environmental assessments before obtaining site approvals. [[entities/association-of-municipalities-of-ontario-entity.md]]

2. **Tax Incentive Framework**
   Municipalities may offer property tax abatements (up to 50% for 5 years) for facilities meeting green energy standards. [[concepts/06-municipal-tax-revenue-concept.md]]

3. **Infrastructure Cost-Sharing**
   Developers are required to fund 100% of electrical substation upgrades and 75% of road widening associated with new facilities. [[entities/quinteconservation-ca-entity.md]]
