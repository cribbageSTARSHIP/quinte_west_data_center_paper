Diesel generators used in data centers emit nitrogen oxides (NOx), linked to respiratory disease, heart disease, and asthma^[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 7]. These generators, though used during emergencies, emit 200-600 times more NOx than natural gas plants, with one Virginia analysis estimating $300M annual public health costs and 14,000 asthma-related impacts^[Source: From Energy Use to Air Quality the Many Ways Data Centers Affect US Communities.pdf, Page 7].

Replacing diesel generators with battery storage or fuel cells can eliminate exhaust emissions, while advanced cooling technologies reduce water use and alleviate local water scarcity^[Source: Global data center expansion and human health- A call for empirical research.pdf, Page 3].
