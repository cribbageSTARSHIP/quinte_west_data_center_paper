Utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) installed at data center substations present critical fire and toxicological hazards, primarily centered on lithium-ion thermal runaway propagation^[Source: 08_hazards_emergency.md].

Once thermal runaway initiates, off-gassing produces explosive hydrogen and toxic hydrofluoric acid ($HF$) vapors^[Source: 08_hazards_emergency.md]. Municipal volunteer and small-city fire departments frequently lack specialized high-volume cooling equipment, specialized suppression agents, and hazmat perimeter infrastructure required to manage multi-megawatt BESS fires safely^[Source: 08_hazards_emergency.md].
