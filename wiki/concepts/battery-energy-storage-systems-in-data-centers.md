# Battery Energy Storage Systems in Data Centers

Battery energy storage systems (BESS) are increasingly adopted as cleaner alternatives to diesel generators for emergency backup power in data centers. Unlike diesel generators, BESS avoid high pollution from PM2.5 and NOx emissions during outages [[1]](https://www.wri.org/insights/us-data-center).

## Key Benefits
- **Zero Emissions**: BESS do not produce NOx or PM2.5 during operation [[2]](https://www.wri.org/insights/us-data-center).
- **Scalability**: Modern lithium-ion BESS can provide energy storage capacity comparable to diesel generators [[3]](https://www.wri.org/insights/us-data-center).
- **Regulatory Alignment**: BESS align with EPA guidelines for reducing PTE in emergency scenarios [[4]](https://www.epa.gov/stationary-source).

## Implementation Challenges
- **Initial Cost**: Battery systems require significant upfront investment compared to diesel generators [[5]](https://www.wri.org/insights/us-data-center).
- **Lifespan**: Lithium-ion batteries degrade over time, requiring periodic replacement [[6]](https://www.wri.org/insights/us-data-center).
