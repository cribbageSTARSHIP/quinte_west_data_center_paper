The data center boom creates opportunities beyond traditional tech sectors, with industrial real estate and telecom networks playing critical roles. Key infrastructure needs include:

1. **Industrial Real Estate**: Companies like Dream Industrial REIT (DIR.UN) provide essential warehousing for cloud companies and hardware suppliers. DIR.UN's 96.8% occupancy rate highlights strong demand for logistics spaces supporting AI infrastructure.

2. **Telecom Connectivity**: TELUS (T) and Quebecor (QBR.B) offer critical network infrastructure. TELUS's Sovereign AI Factories in Quebec and Kamloops demonstrate direct AI infrastructure investment, while Quebecor's broadband growth aligns with rising data center connectivity demands.
