Continuous operation of data center cooling systems and generators produces persistent noise pollution (40-59 decibels), exceeding EPA recommended limits of 55 dBA outdoors and 45 dBA indoors. Long-term exposure correlates with cardiovascular risks, hypertension, tinnitus, and cognitive impairment in children. Studies show noise above 55 dBA (day-night average) leads to poor school performance, increased stress, and behavioral issues. In Virginia, data center noise from 24/7 operations exacerbates these risks for nearby residential populations.^[Source: Health implications of the rapid rise of data centers in Virginia an exploratory assessment.pdf, Page 4]
