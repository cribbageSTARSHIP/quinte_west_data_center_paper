The EPA provides regulatory resources for data centers, including new source performance standards (NSPS) for stationary combustion turbines and stationary engines. These standards set limits on air emissions and hazardous air pollutants (NESHAP) for data center power sources^[Source: Clean Air Act Resources for Data Centers US EPA.pdf, Page 6]^. The EPA encourages data centers to replace diesel backup generators with cleaner technologies like long-duration battery storage to reduce emissions and health impacts^[Source: California data center health impacts tripled in 4 years.pdf, Page 2]^. EPA regional offices offer consultation on compliance and permitting processes for data center emissions^[Source: Clean Air Act Resources for Data Centers US EPA.pdf, Page 4]^.
