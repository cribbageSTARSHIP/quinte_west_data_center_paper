Exposure to 50/60 Hz electromagnetic fields can impact pollinator cognition, including foraging velocity and associative memory. The effects are more pronounced in certain species and under specific environmental conditions.^[Source: paper3.md, Page 117]

Key findings include:
- 50 Hz exposure reduces foraging velocity by 25% in honeybees
- Associative memory retention decreases by 30% at 60 Hz
- Effects are mitigated with 50% reduction in field strength
