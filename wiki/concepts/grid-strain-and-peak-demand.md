The U.S. Department of Energy forecasts data centers will consume 12% of total U.S. electricity by 2028, up from 4.4% in 2023 (^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger..., Page 1]). This represents a 176 terawatt-hour increase in consumption (^[Source: 5 ways data centers endanger..., Page 1]).
