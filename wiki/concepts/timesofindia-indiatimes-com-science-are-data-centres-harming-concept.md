This concept page synthesizes findings from [[entities/ehn-org-honeybee-emf-power-line-pollination-entity.md]]'s 2023 review of anthropogenic electromagnetic field (EMF) impacts on pollinators, with specific focus on honeybees. Key claims:

1. **EMF Exposure Effects**: Lab studies suggest EMFs may interfere with bees' magnetoreception (Earth's magnetic field navigation) and waggle dance communication, leading to reduced foraging efficiency and colony health. `^[Source: Title | https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/are-data-centres-harming-bees-scientists-are-investigating-the-impact-of-electromagnetic-fields/articleshow/132415879.cms]`

2. **Research Gaps**: While 60% of reviewed studies showed statistically significant biological effects (altered behavior, oxidative stress, DNA damage), methodological inconsistencies limit real-world conclusions about data center impacts. `^[Source: Title | https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/are-data-centres-harming-bees-scientists-are-investigating-the-impact-of-electromagnetic-fields/articleshow/132415879.cms]`

3. **Multifactorial Declines**: Bee population declines are attributed to Varroa mites, pesticides, habitat loss, and climate change, not solely EMFs. `^[Source: Title | https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/are-data-centres-harming-bees-scientists-are-investigating-the-impact-of-electromagnetic-fields/articleshow/132415879.cms]`

4. **Conservation Urgency**: 75% of leading food crops depend on pollinators, with 55.6% of U.S. honeybee colonies lost in 2024-25. `^[Source: Title | https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/are-data-centres-harming-bees-scientists-are-investigating-the-impact-of-electromagnetic-fields/articleshow/132415879.cms]`
