Quinte Conservation's watershed management framework focuses on balancing ecological preservation with regional development needs. The organization administers **8 distinct watersheds** across Ontario, including the Salmon River, Napanee River, and Moira River systems. Key priorities include:

- Maintaining **45-460 meter elevation gradients** critical for water flow dynamics
- Managing **population centers** (Trenton, Napanee, Belleville) while protecting **First Nations territories**
- Coordinating with **Highway 401 corridor** development while preserving **Lake Ontario** shoreline ecosystems

The management approach involves:

1. **Watershed boundary delineation** (see map in source document)
2. **Municipal collaboration** with 14 incorporated communities
3. **Ecological monitoring** of 38 identified water bodies
