# Water Use in AI Data Centers

Data centers consume vast quantities of water for cooling, with evaporative systems losing 10-15% through evaporation [[1]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/). A single Google facility used 1B gallons/year from the Missouri River, equivalent to 13,300 households [[2]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/). Water footprints include indirect use in electricity generation and semiconductor manufacturing [[3]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/).

## Environmental Impacts
- Reduced streamflows harm aquatic ecosystems [[4]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/)
- Brine discharge from cooling systems creates local stress [[5]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/)
- PFAS chemicals in cooling systems pose health risks [[6]](https://blog.nwf.org/2026/05/data-centers-water-and-the-strain-on-local-resources/)
