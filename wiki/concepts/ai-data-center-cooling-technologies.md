Modern AI data centers employ advanced cooling technologies to minimize water use and environmental impact. Key developments include:

- [[canadas-data-center-water-policies]]: Waste heat recovery systems that convert data center waste heat into renewable energy (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 8])
- [[02-water-ecology]]: Lenovo's Neptune liquid-cooling technology improves energy efficiency (^[Source: Canadas Responsible Data Centre Development Principles - ised.pdf, Page 11])
- [[04-acoustics-dba-dbc]]: Washington State requires air quality permits for diesel-powered backup generators (^[Source: Diesel pollution from data centers.pdf, Page 1])
