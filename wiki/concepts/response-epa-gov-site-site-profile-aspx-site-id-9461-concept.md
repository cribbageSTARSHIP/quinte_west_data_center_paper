This concept page documents a 2014 propylene glycol spill incident at the Fred Meyer Distribution Center in Clackamas, Oregon, [[entities/fred-meyer-distribution-center-entity.md]]. The spill involved approximately 120 gallons of propylene glycol released into a storm drain leading to Carli Creek, with subsequent cleanup efforts involving vacuum trucks and absorbent materials [[entities/fred-meyer-distribution-center-entity.md]].
