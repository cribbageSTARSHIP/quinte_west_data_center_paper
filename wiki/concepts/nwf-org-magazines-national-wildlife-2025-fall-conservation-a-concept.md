AI data centers require immense energy and water resources while generating air, light, noise, and other pollution. By 2030, global data center electricity use is projected to reach 945 terawatt-hours, with the US hosting the most facilities [[concepts/01-power-grid.md]]. Water consumption can reach 5 million gallons/day per facility, equivalent to a town of 50,000 residents [[concepts/02-water-ecology.md]]. Pollution impacts include air quality degradation from criteria pollutants and potential sensory dangers from light/noise pollution exceeding ecological thresholds. Solutions include energy-efficient hardware upgrades, lower precision computing, and power capping strategies.^[Source: Title | https://www.nwf.org/Magazines/National-Wildlife/2025/Fall/Conservation/AI-Data-Centers]
