A 5,500-gallon diesel fuel spill occurred at the Equinix data center on Hartz Way in Secaucus, NJ, contaminating Anderson Sack Creek. Contractors containing the spill used absorbent booms and skimmers to remove 3,500 gallons of fuel, preventing river contamination. The incident highlights risks of on-site fuel storage for backup generators at data centers, with potential for environmental damage and regulatory fines.^[Source: Title | https://patch.com/new-jersey/secaucus/data-center-spills-5-500-gallons-diesel-secaucus-creek]
