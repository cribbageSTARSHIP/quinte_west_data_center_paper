# Loudoun-Gov-6405-Noise-Air-Quality-Concerns Concept

## Key Noise Characteristics
- Tonal noise from data center machinery (HVAC, generators) produces persistent humming/buzzing at narrow frequency ranges
- Low-frequency noise (below 100 Hz) less audible but may cause health impacts
- DBA measurements miss low-frequency components and tonal discomfort
- dBC measurements better capture low-frequency noise

## Regulatory Challenges
- Current Loudoun County ordinances use dBA thresholds (55 dBA for residential)
- No specific policies for tonal/low-frequency noise
- State law requires 100MW+ facilities to submit noise profiles

## Air Quality Concerns
- Diesel generators emit NOx, PM2.5, CO, SO2
- Tier 2 generators common (some retrofitted with filters)
- Tier 4 generators rare but reduce pollution
- DEQ mandates Tier 4-equivalent standards for new generators
