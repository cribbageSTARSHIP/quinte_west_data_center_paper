This concept page documents the use of **Geocortex Essentials** technology in the CLUPA application, which is part of the Ontario government's environmental and infrastructure planning framework. The software, licensed from Esri® ArcGIS, facilitates geospatial analysis and mapping for decision-making processes related to AI data center siting and impact assessments. However, its integration raises potential hazards related to software reliability and emergency response protocols in rural regions.
