
## Cambridge TOR1 Data Centre Stormwater Retention Pond Discharge (July 2026)
- **Site:** 17 Vondrau Drive, Cambridge, Ontario (Ascent TOR1 Data Centre).
- **Incident:** Uncontrolled discharge of an odorous, milky-white substance into a stormwater management pond adjacent to Maple Grove Road ^[Source: CTV News Kitchener | https://www.ctvnews.ca/kitchener/article/crews-cleaning-up-milky-white-substance-from-stormwater-pond-in-cambridge-ont/].
- **Response & Regulatory Action:** MECP Spills Action Centre took lead jurisdiction over private property containment; Grand River Conservation Authority calculated contaminant travel times for the City of Brantford drinking water intake ^[Source: CambridgeToday | https://www.cambridgetoday.ca/local-news/spill-cleanup-continues-at-cambridge-data-centre-stormwater-pond-12602383].
- **Physical Dynamics:** Site operators attributed the event to an anaerobic bacterial bloom; however, environmental groups highlighted the link between closed-loop chiller fluid leaks (glycol/biocides), extreme Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD), and artificial hypoxia in retention infrastructure.
