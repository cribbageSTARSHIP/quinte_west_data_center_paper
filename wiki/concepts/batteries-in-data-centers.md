# Batteries in Data Centers

Data centers increasingly rely on battery storage systems for backup power and grid stability, particularly as renewable energy integration grows. However, large-scale battery deployments—such as lithium-ion systems—pose new hazards:

- **Thermal runaway risks**: Lithium-ion batteries can overheat and catch fire if improperly managed, as seen in incidents involving BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) [[08-hazards-emergency]].
- **Toxic chemical exposure**: Battery manufacturing and disposal involve heavy metals (e.g., lead, cobalt) and electrolytes that may leak, posing environmental and health risks [[08-hazards-emergency]].
- **Resource extraction impacts**: Mining for battery materials contributes to carbon emissions and ecological degradation [[California data center health impacts tripled in 4 years]].
