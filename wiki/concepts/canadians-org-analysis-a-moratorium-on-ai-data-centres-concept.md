# Moratorium on AI Data Centres

## Key Concerns

### Power Grid Strain
^[Source: https://canadians.org/analysis/a-moratorium-on-ai-data-centres/]
Proposed data centres require significant power, potentially straining Alberta's grid and increasing fossil fuel dependence. For example, Synapse Data Centre Inc. would need 1,400 MW, equivalent to Edmonton's power demand. AESO reports 20,000 MW of data centre load on their project list, far exceeding Alberta's 10,000 MW generation capacity.

### Natural Gas Dependency
^[Source: https://canadians.org/analysis/a-moratorium-on-ai-data-centres/]
Data centres and associated natural gas power plants increase air pollutants, greenhouse gas emissions, and compete for natural gas supply. Meta's 970 MW Sturgeon County data centre plans to operate before its power plant is completed, exacerbating these issues.

### Water Consumption
^[Source: https://canadians.org/analysis/a-moratorium-on-ai-data-centres/]
Data centres and power plants require large water volumes. Wonder Valley's 7,500-MW power plant would consume 10% of Alberta's gas supply, emit 25 million tonnes of CO2, and require significant water for cooling. Power plants with CCS could increase water use by 29-81%.

### Economic Uncertainty
^[Source: https://canadians.org/analysis/a-moratorium-on-ai-data-centres/]
Economic benefits are unclear. Alberta lacks sales/property taxes on data centre equipment, and tax breaks may allow exemptions from municipal taxes for up to 15 years. Comprehensive EIAs are needed to assess full costs and benefits.

## Recommendations

1. Implement a moratorium on hyperscale data centre development until full impacts are understood.
2. Mandate Environmental Impact Assessments (EIAs) for all data centres and infrastructure.
