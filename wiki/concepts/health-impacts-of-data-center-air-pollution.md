### Respiratory Effects
Data center air pollution contributes to 1300 annual deaths in the US and $20B health burden through NOx and PM2.5 emissions [^[Source: raw/papers/08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 684]].

### Cardiovascular Risk
Exposure to PM2.5 from data center generators increases myocardial infarction risk, particularly among women and elderly [^[Source: raw/papers/08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 846]].

### Climate Connection
Data centers account for 2.2% of US CO₂e emissions (105M tons/year), with 90% of historic global emissions from 90 fossil fuel producers [^[Source: raw/papers/08_hazards_emergency.md, Page 852]].

### Noise & Air Pollution Synergy
Residents near data centers report increased health issues from both air pollution and noise pollution [^[Source: 'Living in Hell': Data Center Neighbors Grapple With Noise, Air ... | https://www.usnews.com/news/national-news/articles/2026-04-28/living-in-hell-data-center-neighbors-grapple-with-noise-air-pollution]].
