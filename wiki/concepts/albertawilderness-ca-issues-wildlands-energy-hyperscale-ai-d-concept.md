# Environmental Impact of Hyperscale AI Data Centres

Hyperscale AI data centres in Alberta are facing significant scrutiny due to their environmental, socioeconomic, and health impacts. Key concerns include:

1. **Energy Demand**: Data centres like Synapse (1,400 MW daily) and Meta's 970 MW Sturgeon County facility are straining Alberta's grid, with AESO reporting a 9900% surge in power requests since 2024. This surpasses the province's total generation capacity of 10,000 MW.

2. **Water Use**: Powering these facilities could consume 833 million–5.6 billion litres annually per 1,000 MW, equivalent to 12,000–79,000 Albertans' water use. Fracking for natural gas further depletes water resources and risks contamination.

3. **Pollution**: Natural gas combustion emits methane, worsening air quality and contributing to respiratory illnesses, cardiovascular disease, and premature mortality. CCS technologies could increase water use by 29–81%.

4. **Land Use**: Projects like Wonder Valley (7,500 MW) threaten public lands and agricultural areas, with 25 million tonnes of GHG emissions reversing Alberta's coal phase-out progress.
