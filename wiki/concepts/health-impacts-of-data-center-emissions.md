This page synthesizes findings from Harvard TH Chan School of Public Health analyses of AI data center health impacts. Key findings include:

- Vantage Data Center in Virginia estimated to cause $53M-$99M annual health damages (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 4])
- 3.4-6.5 additional premature deaths/year in impacted regions (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 5])
- Increased asthma-related hospital admissions and respiratory stress (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 5])
- Diesel generators linked to respiratory/cardiovascular diseases through NOx and PM2.5 emissions (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 3])
- California data centers' health impacts tripled 2019-2023 (^[Source: raw/papers/05_human_health.md, Page 7])
