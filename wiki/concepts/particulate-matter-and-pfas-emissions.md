### Particulate Matter and PFAS Emissions from Gas-Powered Data Centers

Gas-powered data centers emit **PM2.5** (fine particulate matter), which enters the bloodstream and contributes to **heart attacks and strokes** (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 2]). They also release **PFAS** (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances), termed "forever chemicals," linked to cancer, birth defects, and immune system weakening (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 3]).

PM2.5 worsens **asthma and COPD**, with **3.4–6.5 premature deaths/year** estimated near high-emission facilities (^[Source: Analyzing air pollution health economic risks from AI data centers Harvard TH Chan School of Public.pdf, Page 5]). PFAS contamination risks are amplified by **leakage from cooling systems** and **long-term environmental persistence** (^[Source: Albertas AI boom poses hidden health environmental risks.pdf, Page 3]).
