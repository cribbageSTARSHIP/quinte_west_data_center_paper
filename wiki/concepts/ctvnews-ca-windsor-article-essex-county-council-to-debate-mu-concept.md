Essex County Council is deliberating a municipal framework to regulate AI data centre development, focusing on land zoning policies and resource impact assessments. The debate, initiated by Kingsville's deputy mayor Kimberly DeYong, aims to proactively address potential applications while balancing economic opportunities with environmental concerns. Key considerations include:

1. **Resource Allocation**: Concerns over water and energy usage require comprehensive planning tools to ensure sustainable operations.
2. **Zoning Regulations**: Potential restrictions on data centres may conflict with other industrial operations, necessitating careful research to avoid unintended economic consequences.
3. **Public Consultation**: Staff will prepare reports on AI data centre requirements, including community engagement strategies.

This initiative aligns with broader Canadian municipal efforts to manage AI infrastructure impacts. ^[Source: CTV News Article | https://www.ctvnews.ca/windsor/article/essex-county-council-to-debate-municipal-framework-for-ai-data-centres/]
